Comentify lets you bring your entire social media team into one shared workspace so everyone can contribute without sharing a single login. Assign roles to match each person's responsibilities, keep sensitive settings locked to the right people, and respond to your audience faster by distributing inbox work across your team.

## Why Team Access Matters

Sharing login credentials creates security risks and makes it impossible to track who made which changes. With Comentify's team access model, every teammate gets their own account under your workspace. You stay in control — granting or revoking access at any time — while your team works independently within the boundaries their role allows.

## Invite a Team Member

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Team Settings">
    Click your workspace name in the bottom-left corner, then select **Settings**. In the Settings menu, choose **Team**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Invite Member">
    On the Team page, click the **Invite Member** button in the top-right corner.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter the Email Address">
    Type the email address of the person you want to invite. Make sure you use the address they'll sign in with — they'll receive their invitation at this address.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose a Role">
    Select the role that matches what this person needs to do. See the [Available Roles](#available-roles) section below if you're unsure which to pick.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Send the Invitation">
    Click **Send Invitation**. The invitee receives an email with a link to accept and set up their account. The invitation expires after 72 hours — resend it from the Team page if they miss it.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Available Roles

Roles define what each team member can see and do inside your Comentify workspace. Assign the most restrictive role that still lets someone do their job.

<CardGroup cols={3}>
  <Card title="Admin" icon="shield-half" href="/team/team-access">
    Full access to all features, including billing, account settings, and team management. Reserve this role for workspace owners and trusted managers.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Manager" icon="briefcase-business" href="/team/team-access">
    Can create, edit, and delete automations, view the analytics dashboard, and manage inbox conversations. Cannot access billing or account-level settings.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Agent" icon="headset" href="/team/team-access">
    Inbox access only. Agents can view and reply to conversations assigned to them but cannot create or edit automations or view analytics data.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

## Manage Existing Team Members

To update a team member's role or remove them from your workspace, go to **Settings > Team**. Find the person in the member list, click the **⋯** menu next to their name, and choose either **Change Role** or **Remove Member**.

- **Change Role** — select the new role from the dropdown and confirm. The change takes effect immediately on their next page load.
- **Remove Member** — removes their access to the workspace instantly. This action cannot be undone; you would need to send a new invitation if you want to re-add them later.

<Note>
  The number of team seats available to your workspace depends on your current plan. If you've reached your seat limit, you'll need to remove an existing member or upgrade your plan before sending a new invitation.
</Note>

## Assign Conversations to Team Members

Once your team is set up, you can route inbox conversations to specific agents or managers directly from the shared inbox. Assigning conversations ensures the right person follows up and prevents duplicate replies. Learn how conversation assignment works in the [Shared Inbox](/inbox/shared-inbox) guide.

<Warning>
  When you remove a team member, any conversations currently assigned to them become unassigned immediately. Unassigned conversations remain visible in the shared inbox, but no one will receive notifications about new replies until you reassign them. Review and reassign open conversations before removing a team member to avoid gaps in your response coverage.
</Warning>
