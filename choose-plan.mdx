Your workspace becomes available after your plan is active.

- If you choose a free plan, your workspace opens right away. You can skip this page. 
- If you choose a paid plan, you see the **Complete Payment** page when you sign in. 
- You pay once on this page, and then your dashboard opens.

## Steps

The payment page has four steps: **Plan**, **Method**, **Payment**, and **Review**.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Choose a payment method" icon="wallet">
    1. Under **Payment Method**, choose how you want to pay.
    2. Click **Continue**.

    {/* Screenshot: Payment page, Payment Method step */}
  </Step>

  <Step title="Apply a coupon (optional)" icon="ticket">
    1. Enter your code in **Coupon Code**.
    2. Click **Apply**.

    If the coupon is valid, you will see the discount in the price summary.

    - **Base amount:** Plan price.
    - **Tax:**  if applicable.
    - **Total payable:** Amount you need to pay.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Complete the payment" icon="credit-card">
    The payment steps depend on your selected method.

    **Razorpay**
    - Click **Pay with Razorpay**.
    - Pay using card, UPI, netbanking, or wallet.
    - Your plan becomes active after payment.

    **Stripe**
    - Click **Pay with Stripe**.
    - Complete the payment on Stripe's payment page.
    - You will return to Comentify after payment.

    **PayPal**
    - Click the PayPal button.
    - Sign in to PayPal or pay by card.
    - Your plan becomes active after payment.

    **Offline Payment (bank transfer)**
    1. Check **Bank Details**.
    2. Transfer the **Total payable** amount.
    3. Enter the bank transaction number in **Transaction ID**.
    4. Upload your payment proof in **Payment screenshot**. This is optional.
    5. Click **Submit for approval**.

    {/* Screenshot: Payment page, Offline Payment Details with Bank Details open */}
  </Step>

  <Step title="Wait for bank transfer approval" icon="hourglass">
    This step applies only to **Offline Payment**.

    - Your payment shows as **Payment waiting for approval**.
    - Our team checks your payment.
    - You receive an email after it is approved.
    - Your workspace opens after approval.

    You can close the page while waiting. Your payment details are saved.

    Payments through **Razorpay, Stripe, or PayPal** do not need manual approval.

    {/* Screenshot: Payment page, Payment waiting for approval */}
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Check>
  Your payment is complete and your workspace is active.
</Check>

## Common questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="My bank transfer was rejected. What should I do?">
    - The page shows **Payment needs resubmission** and the reason for rejection.
    - Correct the payment details and click **Resubmit payment**.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Money was deducted, but my plan is not active.">
    - For Stripe, wait a moment and refresh the page.
    - If you see **Payment verification failed**, contact support with the payment date and amount.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Does my plan renew automatically?">
    - No. Comentify does not charge you automatically.
    - We send reminders 7 days, 3 days, and 1 day before your plan ends.
    - When your plan ends, you can renew it from **Renew Your Subscription**.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can I upgrade my plan later?">
    - Yes. You can upgrade from your settings.
    - Any remaining amount from your current plan is shown as **Plan credit**.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can I pay later?">
    - Yes. Click **Log out** on the payment page.
    - Sign in again when you are ready to pay.
    - You cannot use your workspace until the plan is active.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
