A flow is built with nodes. Each node does one job, such as sending a message or asking a question.

- Add nodes from the node list in the flow builder.
- Nodes are grouped into **Messages** and **Logic**.
- Click a node on the board to edit its settings on the right.

This page explains each node. To learn how to build a flow, see [Flows](/automation/flows).

## Trigger

**What it does:** decides when the flow starts. Every flow has one, and it cannot be deleted.

**Settings:**

* **What starts this flow?:** **Comment → DM**, **DM auto-reply**, **Story reply** or **Story mention**. Story options are Instagram only.
* **When the message…:** **Contains keyword**, **Exact match** or **Any message**.
* **Keywords:** the words that start the flow.
* **Which posts?:** **All posts** or **A specific post** (comment triggers only).
* **Case-sensitive matching:** "Sale" does not match "sale".
* **First interaction only:** start only on the person's first comment or message. Choose what counts under **Counted as their first**.
* **Contact types:** start only for **Lead**, **Guest** or **Customer**. Leave empty for everyone.

<Note>
  If you choose **Not set up yet**, the flow can be published, but it will not start.
</Note>

## Send a message

**What it does:** sends a message and continues to the next step.

**Settings:**

* **Message** (required): write what you want to say. Type `@` to add the person's name.

**Use it for:** greetings, thank-you messages and information.

## Ask a question

**What it does:** asks a question, waits for the person to type an answer, and saves the answer.

**Settings:**

* **Message** (required): write your question.
* **Save the answer as:** a key, which is a short name for the answer, like `city` or `phone`. Comentify stores the person's answer under this key.
  * Use only letters, numbers and `_`.
  * Start with a letter.
  * Give each question its own key, so answers do not mix up.

**Find the answer in the @ picker:**

After you save the key, the answer shows in the merge field picker in this flow.

1. In any later step, type `@` in the message box.
2. Look for `answer.` followed by your key, like `answer.city`.
3. Click it. Comentify adds `{{answer.city}}` to your message.

When the flow runs, Comentify swaps `{{answer.city}}` for what the person answer.

<Note>
  The picker shows one `answer.` entry for every **Ask a question** step in the flow. If your flow has a **Collect an email** step, you also see `answer.email`.
</Note>

**Where you can use the answer:**

* **In a message:** `Great, we deliver to {{answer.city}}!`
* **In Update the contact:** put `{{answer.phone}}` in **Phone** to save the answer on the contact.
* **In Branch on an answer:** choose `city` in **Look at** to pick a path based on the answer.

**Example:** "Which city are you in?" → **Save the answer as** `city` → next message: `Great, we deliver to {{answer.city}}!`

## Buttons

**What it does:** shows up to 3 buttons, waits for a selection and follows that button's path.

**Settings:**

* **Message above the buttons** (required): the text the person sees above the buttons, like "Do you want the price list?"
* Click **Add** to add a button. You can add up to 3.

**Settings for each button:**

* **Label:** the button text, such as "Yes, please". Up to 20 characters.
* **Value:** the name of the path, such as `YES`.

Each button has its own line on the board. Connect each line to the next step.

**Example:** "Do you want the price list?" → **Yes** / **No**

## Collect an email

**What it does:** asks for an email, waits for it and saves it to the person's contact.

**Settings:**

* **Message** (required): your question, such as "What's your email? I'll send the guide there."

**After this step:**

* The email is saved on the contact in **Contacts**.
* Use `{{answer.email}}` in later messages.

## Require a follow

**What it does:** checks if the person follows your Instagram account and follows the correct path.

**Settings:**

* **Message** (required): tell the person why they should follow you. They see it if they do not follow you yet.
* **Button label:** the button they tap after following, such as "I've followed". Tapping it checks again.
* **Visit profile button** (optional): opens your Instagram profile.
* **Extra link button** (optional): opens a web page. The link must start with `https://`.

<Note>
  This step works only on Instagram. Meta does not provide follow details for Facebook Pages.
</Note>

**Tip:** In **Try it**, click **Pretend they followed** to test the **Follows** path.

## Send media

**What it does:** sends a photo, video or audio file and continues.

**Settings:**

* Upload your file.
* **Caption** (optional): text sent with the file. Meta sends it as a separate message after the file.

## Quick replies

**What it does:** shows up to 13 choices. The person taps one and the flow follows its path.

**Settings:**

* **Message above the quick replies** (required): the text the person sees above the choices, like "Which size do you need?"
* Add up to 13 choices.

**Settings for each choice:**

* **Label:** the text shown to the person. Up to 20 characters.
* **Value:** the name of the path.

The choices disappear after the person selects one.

## Send a link button

**What it does:** sends buttons that open a web page. The flow continues immediately.

**Settings:**

* **Message above the buttons** (required): the text the person sees above the link buttons.
* Add link buttons with a label, such as "Shop now", and a link. Links must start with `https://`.

**Use it for:** shop links, booking pages and downloads.

## Branch on an answer

**What it does:** looks at what the person typed earlier, and sends them down one of two paths: **Yes** or **No**.

Think of it as a question Comentify asks itself: *"Did the person's answer match what I am looking for?"*

- If it matches, the flow goes down the **Yes** path.
- If it does not match, the flow goes down the **No** path.

### Before you use it

You need an **Ask a question** step earlier in the flow. **Branch on an answer** checks the answer that step saved.

### Settings

Read the three boxes together, like one sentence: **Look at** `city` **Which** `is exactly` **This** `Mumbai`.

| Box | What to choose | Example |
|---|---|---|
| **Look at** | Which saved answer to check. You see the keys from your **Ask a question** steps. | `city` |
| **Which** | How to compare. See the table below. | `is exactly` |
| **This** | The word to compare with. | `Mumbai` |

### The choices in "Which"

| Choice | Goes down **Yes** when… | Example: the person typed "I live in Mumbai" |
|---|---|---|
| **contains** | the answer has your word anywhere in it. | **This** `Mumbai` → **Yes** |
| **is exactly** | the answer is only your word, nothing more. | **This** `Mumbai` → **No**, because the answer has more words |
| **is not** | the answer is anything other than your word. | **This** `Mumbai` → **Yes** |
| **is empty** | the person gave no answer. | → **No** |
| **is not empty** | the person gave any answer. | → **Yes** |

For **is empty** and **is not empty**, leave **This** blank. There is nothing to compare.

<Tip>
  Capital letters do not matter. "YES", "Yes" and "yes" all match `yes`.
</Tip>

<Tip>
  People type in many ways. Use **contains** instead of **is exactly** when you can. For example, **contains** `yes` matches "yes", "Yes please" and "yes!!".
</Tip>

### Join the two paths

On the board, this step has two lines: **Yes** and **No**. Join each line to the step that should come next. Both lines must be joined before you can publish.

### Example

1. **Ask a question:** "Which city are you in?" → **Save the answer as** `city`.
2. **Branch on an answer:** **Look at** `city`, **Which** `contains`, **This** `Mumbai`.
3. **Yes path → Send a message:** "Great news, we deliver in Mumbai today!"
4. **No path → Send a message:** "We deliver to your city in 3 to 5 days."

## Update the contact

**What it does:** updates the person's details in **Contacts**.

**Settings:** fill in only the details you want to change:

* **First name**, **Last name**, **Email**, **Phone**
* **Type**, **Status**, **Source**
* **Groups:** adds the person to these groups. It does not remove them from other groups.

Empty fields stay unchanged.

**Example:** after **Collect an email**, set **Type** to **Lead** and **Status** to "Interested".

## Assign the chat

**What it does:** assigns the chat and contact to a team member.

**Settings:**

* Choose the team member.

<Note>
  If the flow started from a comment and the person has not replied yet, there is no chat to assign. Comentify skips this step.
</Note>

## Wait

**What it does:** pauses the flow before moving to the next step.

**Settings:**

* **Wait for:** choose how long to wait. The maximum is 23 hours, 59 minutes and 59 seconds.

<Note>
  Meta allows messages only within 24 hours of the person's last message.
</Note>

## Hand over to a person

**What it does:** stops the flow, pauses automatic replies and marks the chat **Needs you** in **Chat**.

**Settings:** none.

**Tip:** Add a **Send a message** step before it, such as "Thanks! A team member will reply soon."

Learn more: [Chat](/inbox/shared-inbox#when-a-person-needs-you)

## End

**What it does:** stops the flow.

**Settings:** none.

Every path in your flow should end with **End** or **Hand over to a person**.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Trigger" icon="play">
    **Comment → DM**, keyword `guide`.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Ask a question" icon="message-circle-question-mark">
    "Want my free guide? Reply YES!" Save the answer as `reply`.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Collect an email" icon="mail">

  </Step>
  <Step title="Update the contact" icon="user-pen">

  </Step>

  </Step>
  <Step title="End" icon="circle-stop">
    The flow stops.
  </Step>
</Steps>
