Comentify's comment reply automation lets you respond to your audience instantly — without manually monitoring every post. By defining keyword triggers and pre-written messages, you can keep conversations moving, answer common questions, and boost engagement around the clock.

## What Is Comment Reply Automation?

Comment reply automation posts a public reply on your behalf whenever a comment matches a rule you define. Instead of watching for every "How much does this cost?" or "Where can I buy it?", you set the rule once and Comentify handles the rest.

This is especially useful for:

- **Product launches** — answer pricing and availability questions automatically
- **Giveaways and contests** — confirm entries and share instructions instantly
- **Always-on support** — respond to FAQs even outside business hours
- **High-volume posts** — keep up with comment floods on viral or boosted content

## Create a Comment Reply Rule

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Comment Replies">
    In the Comentify dashboard, navigate to **Automations** → **Comment Replies** and click **New Rule**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select the Target Post">
    Choose which post the rule applies to. Select a specific post from your connected Instagram or Facebook account, or toggle **Apply to all posts** to cover every post on that account.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set Keyword Triggers (Optional)">
    Under **Trigger**, choose whether the rule fires on **Any comment** or only when a comment **contains specific keywords**. If you choose keywords, enter each one and configure the match type (see [Keyword Triggers](#keyword-triggers) below).

    Leave this set to **Any comment** if you want to reply to every comment regardless of content.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Write the Reply Message">
    In the **Reply** field, type the message Comentify will post publicly. You can use personalization variables such as `{first_name}` to address commenters by name. See [Reply Message Customization](#reply-message-customization) for all available variables.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Activate the Rule">
    Toggle the rule to **Active** and click **Save Rule**. Comentify will begin monitoring for matching comments immediately.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Keyword Triggers

Keyword triggers let you fire a reply only when a comment includes a specific word or phrase. This gives you precise control over which comments receive an automated response.

### Match Types

| Match Type | Behavior |
|---|---|
| **Contains** | Fires if the keyword appears anywhere in the comment. `"price"` matches `"what's the price?"` |
| **Exact match** | Fires only if the entire comment equals the keyword. `"info"` matches `"info"` but not `"send info please"` |

### Case Sensitivity

All keyword matching is **case-insensitive** by default. The keyword `"Info"` will match `"info"`, `"INFO"`, and `"Info"` equally.

### Multiple Keywords

You can add multiple keywords to a single rule. The rule fires if **any one** of the keywords matches — you do not need a comment to contain all of them. To add another keyword, click **+ Add Keyword** in the trigger section.

If you need different replies for different keywords, create separate rules for each keyword group.

## Reply Message Customization

Make your automated replies feel personal by using dynamic variables in your message text. Comentify replaces these placeholders with real values at the time the reply is posted.

| Variable | Replaced With |
|---|---|
| `{first_name}` | The commenter's first name |

**Example:**
```
Hey {first_name}, thanks for your comment! Check the link in our bio for full details. 🙌
```

If the commenter's name is unavailable (for example, on certain privacy settings), Comentify omits the variable gracefully rather than displaying the raw placeholder.

<Tip>
  Keep public replies short, warm, and action-oriented. A one- or two-sentence reply that directs the commenter to your bio link or invites them to check their DMs performs significantly better than a long block of text. Save the detail for a follow-up DM.
</Tip>

<Note>
  Automated comment replies must comply with [Meta's Community Standards and Platform Policies](https://transparency.meta.com/policies/community-standards/). Avoid spammy, repetitive, or misleading replies — accounts that violate these guidelines may have automation features restricted. Review Meta's policies before launching high-volume rules.
</Note>

## Manage Your Rules

All your comment reply rules live in **Automations** → **Comment Replies**. From the rules list you can:

- **Enable / Disable** — Toggle the switch next to any rule to pause or resume it without deleting it.
- **Edit** — Click the rule name to open the editor and update the target post, keywords, or message.
- **Delete** — Open the rule and click **Delete Rule** at the bottom of the editor. Deleted rules cannot be recovered.

Use the **Status** filter at the top of the list to view only active or inactive rules, and the **Search** bar to find rules by name when your list grows large.
