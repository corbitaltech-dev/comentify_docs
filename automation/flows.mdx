A **flow** is a visual builder for creating step-by-step conversations.

* An automation sends one reply and stops.
* A flow can ask a question, wait for an answer and take different actions.
* You build a flow by adding steps and connecting them.

For example:

1. Someone comments **guide** on your post.
2. The flow sends a DM: "Want the free guide? Reply YES."
3. The person replies "YES".
4. The flow asks for their email and saves it.
5. The flow sends the guide link.

## Flow or automation?

| You want to…                             | Use                                      |
| ---------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------- |
| Send one reply to a comment or message.  | An [automation](/automation/automations) |
| Post a public reply under a comment.     | An automation (**Comment auto-reply**)   |
| Ask a question and wait for the answer.  | A flow                                   |
| Show buttons and take different actions. | A flow                                   |
| Collect an email and phone number.       | A flow                                   |
| Check a follow, then ask for an email.   | A flow                                   |
| Hand the chat to your team.              | A flow                                   |

<Tip>
  Use an automation for simple replies. Use a flow when you need multiple steps or answers.
</Tip>

## Build your first flow

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create the flow" icon="plus">
    1. In the left menu, click **Flows**.
    2. Click **Add flow**.
    3. In **New flow**, enter a **Name** and choose a **Channel**.
    4. Click **Save**.
    
    The flow builder opens. The flow is off until you publish it.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Set up the trigger" icon="play">
    The **Trigger** step decides what starts the flow.
    Click it and fill in:
    - **What starts this flow?:** choose **Comment → DM**, **DM auto-reply**, **Story reply** or **Story mention**.
    - **When the message…:** choose **Contains keyword**, **Exact match** or **Any message**.
    - **Keywords:** enter your keyword, like `guide`.
    - **Which posts?:** choose **All posts** or **A specific post**.

    Learn more about these options in [Automations](/automation/automations).
  </Step>

  <Step title="Add steps" icon="list-plus">
    - Open the step list. Steps are in two groups: **Messages** and **Logic**.
    - Click a step, or drag it onto the board.
    - Click a step on the board to edit its details.

    See all steps in [Flow steps](/automation/flow-steps).
  </Step>

  <Step title="Join the steps" icon="spline">
    - Drag a line from one step to the next.
    - Steps with choices, like **Buttons**, have one line for each choice.
    - Finish every path with an **End** or **Hand over to a person** step.

  Click **Rearrange** to organize the board.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Test it with Try it" icon="flask-conical">
    Click **Try it** to test the flow.
    - It **Runs the draft. Nothing is sent.**
    - Type in **Reply as the contact…**
    - Click **Pretend they followed** to test a **Require a follow** step.
    - Click **Restart from the trigger** to test again from the beginning.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Save and publish" icon="rocket">
    1. Click **Save**.
    2. Go back to **Flows**.
    3. Turn on the **Published** switch next to your flow.
  You see **This flow is now answering people.**
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Check>
  You created a flow, set its trigger, added and connected steps, tested it with **Try it**, and published it.
</Check>

## Fix problems before you save

The builder checks your flow while you work. A step with a problem shows a warning, such as **2 fields to fill in** or **1 branch to connect**. You must fix these problems before saving. The **Save** button says **Fix the highlighted steps first**.

| Message                                              | What to do                                                 |
| ---------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------- |
| **This node leads nowhere.**                         | Connect this step to the next one, or add an **End** step. |
| **Nothing leads to this node, so it can never run.** | Connect another step to it, or delete it.                  |
| **The flow needs a trigger to start from.**          | Set up the **Trigger** step.                               |

## Rules for flows that start from a comment

Meta allows only one plain-text private reply to each comment. So a flow with a **Comment → DM** trigger must:

* start with a plain-text **Send a message** or **Ask a question** step, with no buttons, media or links.
* send only one message before waiting for the person to reply.

After the person replies, the flow can send buttons, media and other message types.

<Tip>
  A good first message: "Thanks for your comment! Reply YES and I'll send you the link." After the person replies, the flow can continue with buttons, media and other steps.
</Tip>

## How flows behave

* **One flow at a time.** A person can only be in one flow at a time.
* **Waiting for an answer.** If the person does not reply within 24 hours, the flow stops.
* **When you reply yourself.** If a team member replies in **Chat**, the flow pauses for that chat for 24 hours.
* **Changes go live at once.** When you save a published flow, the changes are active immediately. Use **Try it** before saving.
* **Ice breakers.** An ice breaker can start a published flow. Learn more in [Ice breakers](/channels/ice-breakers).

## Manage your flows

| What you want to do | How to do it                                                                         |
| ------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| Turn on or off      | Use the **Published** switch in the list.                                            |
| Change it           | Click the flow name to open the builder.                                             |
| Delete it           | Click the menu (⋮), then delete. People in the middle of the flow will not continue. |
| Find one            | Use the filters: **Trigger**, **Published** or **Not currently running**.            |

When you turn off a flow, you see **It has stopped answering, and its keywords are free again.** Other automations and flows can now use its keywords.

A **Not running** badge means the flow is published but cannot run. For example, its channel may have stopped working, or it may have no trigger.

## Common questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Why did my flow not start?">
    - Check that the **Published** switch is on.
    - Check that the trigger is set up and the keyword matches.
    - Check that the person is not already in another flow.
    - Check that a team member did not reply in **Chat** in the last 24 hours.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can an automation and a flow use the same keyword?">
    - Not for the same trigger type on the same channel.
    - Comentify will stop you and show the problem. Learn more in [When a keyword is already in use](/automation/keyword-in-use).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Why can I not choose Comment auto-reply as a trigger?">
    - It send public replies, which cannot start a private chat.
    - Use an automation for them.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Why can I not choose My next post?">
    - **My next post** and **Every post from now on** are for automations only.
    - In a flow, choose **All posts** or **A specific post**.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does Try it use credits?">
    - No. **Try it** is only a test and sends nothing to Meta.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
