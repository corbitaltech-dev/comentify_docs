Responding to dozens — or hundreds — of DMs every day is time-consuming, especially when each message deserves a thoughtful, human-sounding reply. Comentify's AI reply suggestions read the context of an open conversation and generate a ready-to-send draft in seconds, so your team spends less time writing from scratch and more time building real connections with your audience.

## How AI Reply Suggestions Work

When you're viewing a conversation in the Shared Inbox, Comentify's AI analyzes the message thread — including the customer's most recent message and the prior exchange — to generate a contextually relevant reply suggestion. The suggestion appears directly in the reply composer, where you can edit it freely before sending. Nothing is sent automatically; you stay in full control at every step.

## Use AI Replies in the Shared Inbox

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open a Conversation">
    Navigate to the **Shared Inbox** and select the Instagram or Facebook DM conversation you want to reply to.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click the AI Suggest Button">
    In the reply composer at the bottom of the conversation, click the **AI Suggest** button (represented by a sparkle or wand icon). Comentify will analyze the thread and generate a suggested reply.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review the Suggestion">
    Read the generated suggestion carefully. Check that it accurately addresses the customer's message, matches your brand tone, and includes any specific information they asked for.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Edit as Needed">
    Click anywhere in the reply composer to edit the suggestion. Add personal details, correct anything that doesn't fit, or rewrite sections to better reflect your voice.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Send Your Reply">
    When you're happy with the message, click **Send**. The reply is delivered to the customer on Instagram or Facebook, just like any manually written message.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  Always review AI suggestions before sending. The AI generates replies based on conversation context, but it doesn't know your business specifics — things like order numbers, pricing details, or recent promotions may need to be added manually to make the reply accurate and personal.
</Tip>

## Tips for Better AI Suggestions

Getting the most out of AI replies is straightforward once you know what influences the output.

- **Keep the conversation thread clean.** The AI reads recent messages to build context. If earlier messages in the thread are vague or off-topic, the suggestion may be less accurate.
- **Apply any available tone or style settings.** If your inbox offers options to set the communication style, select the one that best matches your brand before clicking AI Suggest.
- **Be specific in your conversations.** The more clearly the customer states their question or issue, the more targeted the AI suggestion will be. If needed, ask a clarifying follow-up before generating a suggestion.
- **Regenerate when needed.** If the first suggestion misses the mark, click **AI Suggest** again to get a fresh draft based on the same context.

## Use AI When Composing Automation Messages

AI assistance isn't limited to live conversations — you can also use it when building DM automations and workflow sequences.

When you create or edit an automation in the **Automation** section, look for the **AI Assist** button inside the message editor. Click it to generate a message draft based on the automation's purpose — for example, a welcome message, a promotional follow-up, or an FAQ response. Refine the generated copy to match your campaign goals, then save it to your workflow.

<Note>
  AI reply suggestions and AI-assisted automation messages are available on eligible plans. If you don't see the AI Suggest button in your inbox or automation editor, check your plan or contact Comentify support to learn more about access.
</Note>
