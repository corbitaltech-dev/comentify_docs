Comentify's built-in AI capabilities take the guesswork out of social media engagement. Whether you're responding to a flood of Instagram DMs or building automated workflows for Facebook, AI assistance helps your team craft on-brand, thoughtful messages in far less time — without sacrificing the personal touch your audience expects.

## What AI Can Do for You

Comentify surfaces AI assistance at the two moments that matter most: when you're replying to a live conversation and when you're composing an automated message.

### AI Reply Suggestions in the Shared Inbox

When you open a conversation in the Shared Inbox, Comentify can analyze the thread context and instantly generate a suggested reply. Instead of staring at a blank text box, you get a ready-to-edit starting point that fits the tone and topic of the conversation. Your team can review, refine, and send — all without leaving the inbox.

### AI Assistance for Automation Messages

When building DM automations or workflow sequences, the AI assist tool helps you generate compelling message copy on demand. Describe the goal of your message, let AI draft it, then tailor it to match your brand voice before saving it to your automation.

## Data and Privacy

Comentify uses AI to process the text of your conversations in order to generate reply and message suggestions. This processing is limited to producing suggestions within the product — **Comentify does not use your conversation data to train AI models**. Your customer data remains yours. For full details, refer to the Comentify Privacy Policy.
