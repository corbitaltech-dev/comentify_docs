## Account & Getting Started

<Accordion title="What social platforms does Comentify support?">
  Comentify currently supports **Instagram Professional accounts** (Creator and Business profiles) and **Facebook Pages**. Personal Instagram profiles and Facebook personal timelines are not supported because Meta's API only grants the necessary permissions to professional account types.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Do I need a developer account to use Comentify?">
  No. Comentify connects to Instagram and Facebook through a standard OAuth flow — you simply log in with your Facebook credentials and grant the required permissions. You do not need to create a Meta developer app or go through any technical setup.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Can I use Comentify with a personal Instagram profile?">
  No. Meta's API restricts comment and messaging access to **Instagram Professional accounts** only. If you currently have a personal profile, you can switch it to a Professional account for free inside the Instagram app under **Settings → Account → Switch to Professional Account** — this does not delete any of your existing content or followers.
</Accordion>

## Automation

<Accordion title="How quickly does automation trigger after a comment?">
  In most cases, Comentify detects a new comment and triggers the automation **within seconds**. The exact speed depends on Meta's webhook delivery time, which is typically near-instant but can occasionally take up to a minute during periods of high load on Meta's infrastructure.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Can I use automations on old posts?">
  Yes — as long as the Instagram or Facebook account is actively connected to Comentify, automations apply to **all posts on that account**, including ones published before you set up the automation. Any new comment left on an old post will trigger the relevant automation just as it would on a new post.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Is there a limit on how many automations I can create?">
  Yes, the number of active automations depends on your plan. Each plan tier has its own automation limit, and higher-tier plans offer more — up to unlimited on Business and Enterprise. If you reach your plan's limit, you'll need to either deactivate an existing automation or upgrade your plan before creating a new one. See the [Plans](/account/plans) page for a full feature comparison, or visit [comentify.io/pricing](https://comentify.io/pricing) for current details.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="What happens if Meta's API is down?">
  If Meta experiences an outage or degrades their API, Comentify cannot receive webhook events or send DMs during that window. Any comments made while the API is unavailable **will not retroactively trigger automations** once service is restored. You can monitor Meta's platform status at [developers.facebook.com/status](https://developers.facebook.com/status). Our team also posts updates on service disruptions to the Comentify status page.
</Accordion>

## Billing & Plans

<Accordion title="Can I try Comentify before paying?">
  Yes — Comentify offers a free trial so you can explore the platform before committing to a paid plan. Visit [comentify.io/pricing](https://comentify.io/pricing) to see current trial terms and start your free trial. No credit card is required to begin.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="What happens to my data if I cancel?">
  When you cancel, your plan remains active until the end of the current billing period. After that, your workspace enters a **read-only state** — automations stop running, but your data (conversation history, leads, and analytics) is retained for **90 days**. During that window you can log in and export anything you need. After 90 days, your data is permanently deleted. If you reactivate within the 90-day window, everything is restored exactly as you left it.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Can I change plans at any time?">
  Yes. You can upgrade or downgrade your plan at any time from **Settings → Billing**. Upgrades take effect immediately and are prorated for the remainder of your billing cycle. Downgrades take effect at the end of the current billing period — you keep your existing plan's features until then. Be aware that downgrading to a plan with lower limits may pause automations if your usage exceeds the new plan's caps. See the [Plans](/account/plans) page for details.
</Accordion>

## Troubleshooting

<Accordion title="My automations stopped working — what should I check?">
  Work through this checklist in order:

  1. **Check the connection status** — Go to **Settings → Connected Accounts** and confirm the affected Instagram or Facebook account shows as **Active**. If it shows as expired or errored, reconnect it.
  2. **Check automation status** — Go to **Automations** and confirm the automation is toggled **On**. Automations can be automatically paused if you exceed your plan's limits.
  3. **Review your plan limits** — If you've hit your automation or account limit, some automations may have been paused. Upgrade your plan or deactivate unused automations.
  4. **Check Meta's API status** — Visit [developers.facebook.com/status](https://developers.facebook.com/status) to see if there is an ongoing outage.
  5. **Review automation error logs** — Click into the automation and open the **Activity** tab to see a log of recent trigger attempts and any error messages.

  If none of the above resolves the issue, contact [support@comentify.io](mailto:support@comentify.io) with your workspace name and the name of the affected automation.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="I'm not receiving DMs from comment triggers — what should I check?">
  If your automation is triggering correctly but the DM isn't being delivered, check the following:

  - **Instagram's 24-hour messaging window** — Instagram only allows businesses to send a DM to a user if that user has messaged your account within the last 24 hours, *or* if they have explicitly opted in (for example, by commenting with a keyword that triggers an automation reply). If the user has never interacted with your account via DM and the 24-hour window has closed, Instagram's API will block the message delivery.
  - **User's DM settings** — Some users restrict who can send them DMs. If their privacy settings block messages from accounts they don't follow, the DM will fail silently.
  - **Connected account permissions** — Confirm that Comentify has the necessary messaging permissions for your account. If you're unsure, reconnect the account from **Settings → Connected Accounts** to re-grant all required permissions.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="A team member can't see the inbox — what should I check?">
  Inbox visibility is controlled by **role permissions**. Check the following:

  1. Go to **Settings → Team** and confirm the team member has been added to the workspace.
  2. Review the role assigned to them. The **Viewer** role does not include inbox access — they need at least the **Agent** role to view and respond to conversations.
  3. If inbox access is limited to specific accounts or inboxes, confirm the team member has been assigned to the relevant accounts under **Settings → Team → [Member Name] → Account Access**.

  If the issue persists after checking permissions, ask the team member to sign out and sign back in to refresh their session.
</Accordion>
