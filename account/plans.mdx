Comentify offers tiered subscription plans designed to scale with your business — whether you're a solo creator just getting started or a growing agency managing social engagement across multiple brands. Each plan unlocks a different set of features, account limits, and team capabilities, so you only pay for what you actually need.

## How to Upgrade Your Plan

Upgrading takes effect immediately — your new limits and features are available as soon as the payment is confirmed.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Settings">
    Click your workspace avatar in the bottom-left corner, then select **Settings**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Go to Billing">
    In the left sidebar, click **Billing**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose a Plan">
    Under **Current Plan**, click **Upgrade Plan**. Review the available plans and select the one that fits your needs.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm Payment">
    Enter or confirm your payment details and click **Confirm Upgrade**. You'll receive a confirmation email once the charge is processed.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## How to Cancel Your Subscription

You can cancel your subscription at any time from the Billing page. Your plan remains active until the end of the current billing period — you won't be charged again after that, and you won't lose access to your data immediately.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Settings">
    Click your workspace avatar and select **Settings**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Go to Billing">
    Click **Billing** in the sidebar.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Cancel Subscription">
    Scroll to **Danger Zone** and click **Cancel Subscription**. Confirm your choice in the dialog that appears.
  </Step>
</Steps>

After cancellation, your account switches to a read-only state at the end of the billing period. Automations stop running, but your data — including conversation history, leads, and analytics — is retained for 90 days so you can export it before it is permanently deleted.

<Warning>
  **Downgrading to a lower plan?** If your current usage exceeds the limits of the plan you're moving to — for example, you have more connected accounts than the target plan allows — some automations may be automatically paused until you reduce your usage to within the new limits. Review your usage before downgrading to avoid unexpected disruptions.
</Warning>

## Billing FAQ

<Accordion title="When am I charged?">
  You are charged at the start of each billing cycle. If you upgrade mid-cycle, you are charged a prorated amount for the remainder of the current period, and your full new plan rate applies from the next renewal date.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Can I switch plans mid-cycle?">
  Yes. Upgrades take effect immediately and are prorated. Downgrades take effect at the end of the current billing period — you keep your existing plan's features until then.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Do you offer refunds?">
  Comentify does not offer refunds for partial billing periods. If you cancel, you retain access to your current plan until the end of the period you've already paid for. If you believe you were charged in error, contact [support@comentify.io](mailto:support@comentify.io) within 14 days of the charge.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="What payment methods are accepted?">
  Comentify accepts all major credit and debit cards (Visa, Mastercard, American Express). Enterprise customers can request invoice-based billing — contact [sales@comentify.io](mailto:sales@comentify.io) to arrange this.
</Accordion>
