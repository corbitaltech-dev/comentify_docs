The Comentify Settings dashboard is your central hub for managing everything about your account and workspace. From here you can update personal details, control how Comentify notifies you, manage the social accounts you've connected, and keep your workspace organised for your team.

## Profile Settings

Your profile settings control how you appear inside Comentify and how you sign in.

To open your profile settings, click your workspace avatar in the bottom-left corner and select **Settings**, then choose **Profile** from the sidebar.

- **Display name** — Update the name shown to teammates in the inbox and assignment notifications. Click the name field, type your new name, and click **Save Changes**.
- **Email address** — Change the email address associated with your Comentify account. See the note below about re-verification.
- **Password** — Update your password from the **Security** section. See [Security](#security) below.

<Note>
  Changing your email address triggers a verification email to the new address. Your old address remains active for sign-in until you click the confirmation link in that email. Check your spam folder if the email doesn't arrive within a few minutes.
</Note>

## Notification Preferences

Comentify can send you email notifications for key events so you stay on top of activity without having to keep the app open. Navigate to **Settings → Notifications** to customise which alerts you receive.

| Notification | What it means |
|---|---|
| **New lead captured** | A contact submitted a lead capture form linked to one of your automations. |
| **Conversation assigned to you** | A teammate assigned an inbox conversation to you for follow-up. |
| **Automation error** | An automation failed to trigger or send a DM — usually due to a permission change on the connected account. |

Toggle each notification type on or off using the switches on the Notifications page, then click **Save Preferences**.

## Connected Accounts

The **Connected Accounts** section lists every Instagram Professional account and Facebook Page currently linked to your Comentify workspace. From here you can:

- See the connection status of each account (active, expired, or error).
- Reconnect an account whose access token has expired.
- Remove an account from your workspace.

For a full walkthrough of linking and managing social accounts, see the [Connecting Accounts](/connecting-accounts) guide.

## Workspace Settings

Workspace settings apply to your entire Comentify workspace and are visible to all members with admin access. Navigate to **Settings → Workspace** to manage them.

- **Workspace name** — Update the name shown in the top-left corner of the app and in billing emails.
- **Timezone** — Set the timezone used for analytics date ranges, scheduled reports, and automation scheduling.

<Tip>
  Set your workspace timezone to match your local time — or the time zone of the audience you're targeting. Analytics date ranges, daily summary emails, and any scheduled automations all use this setting. If the timezone is wrong, your reports may show data that appears to span the wrong days.
</Tip>

## Security

Keep your account secure by using a strong, unique password and reviewing your active sessions regularly.

### Changing Your Password

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Security Settings">
    Go to **Settings → Security**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter Your Current Password">
    Type your current password in the **Current password** field to confirm your identity.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set a New Password">
    Enter your new password and confirm it in the **Confirm new password** field. Passwords must be at least 8 characters and include a mix of letters and numbers.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    Click **Update Password**. You'll receive a confirmation email at your registered address.
  </Step>
</Steps>

### Active Sessions

The **Active Sessions** list shows every device and browser currently signed in to your account, including the approximate location and last active time. If you see a session you don't recognise, click **Revoke** next to it to sign that device out immediately. To sign out of all other devices at once, click **Revoke All Other Sessions**.
