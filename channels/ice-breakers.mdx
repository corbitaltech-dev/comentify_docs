Ice breakers are ready-made questions that show in your chat.

- A new person opens a chat with you and sees your questions.
- They tap a question instead of typing.
- Comentify answers them right away.

For example, a person taps **"What are your opening hours?"** and gets your opening hours at once.

## Add an ice breaker

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Ice Breakers" icon="list">
    1. In the left menu, click **Channels**.
    2. Click the menu (⋮) next to your channel.
    3. Click **Ice Breakers**.

    The **Ice Breakers** panel opens. It shows how many you have used, like **1 / 4 used**.

    {/* Screenshot: Ice Breakers panel with the list and the Add ice breaker button */}
  </Step>

  <Step title="Click Add ice breaker" icon="plus">
    Click **Add ice breaker**.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Write your question" icon="message-circle-question-mark">
    - Type the **Question**.
    - You can use up to 80 characters.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Choose what happens when someone taps it" icon="split">
    Under **When tapped**, choose one option.

    **Send a message**
    - Write your answer in **Reply**.
    - Type `@` to add the person's name, like `{{first_name}}`.

    **Start a flow**
    - Choose a flow from the list.
    - The flow starts when contact tap on that.

    {/* Screenshot: Add ice breaker form with Question, When tapped and Reply */}
  </Step>

  <Step title="Save" icon="save">
    - Click **Save**.
    - Comentify sends your ice breaker to Meta.
    - When it shows **Live on Meta**, people can see it in your chat.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Check>
  You opened **Ice Breakers** on your channel, added a question, chose a reply or a flow, and saved it.
</Check>

## Manage your ice breakers

| What you want to do | How to do it |
|---|---|
| Change the order | Drag a question up or down. Meta shows them in the same order. |
| Hide one for now | Turn off its switch. It stays saved but is **Not shown in chat**. |
| Change one | Click **Edit** on that question. |
| Remove one | Click **Delete**, then confirm. You cannot undo this. |

### What each status means

| Status | What it means |
|---|---|
| **Live on Meta** | Your ice breakers show in the chat. |
| **Publishing** | Comentify is sending your changes to Meta. |
| **Failed** | Meta did not accept your last change. Read the message under **Meta rejected the last update**, fix the question, and save again. |

## Good to know

- You can add up to **4** ice breakers on each channel. This is a Meta rule.
- Ice breakers show only to people who have not messaged you yet.
- Ice breakers do not show on Instagram in a computer browser. People see them in the Instagram app.
- Each channel has its own ice breakers. Add them to each channel you want.

## Common questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Why can I not add a fifth ice breaker?">
    - Meta allows only 4 ice breakers on each channel.
    - Delete or change an old one to add a new question.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Why do I not see my ice breakers on Instagram?">
    - Check that the status says **Live on Meta**.
    - Check that the switch next to the question is on.
    - Open the chat in the Instagram app. Ice breakers do not show on a computer.
    - Use an account that has never messaged you. People who already chatted with you do not see them.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Why is Start a flow empty?">
    - This channel has no published flows yet.
    - Publish a flow on this channel first, or choose **Send a message** instead.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I see who tapped an ice breaker?">
    - Yes. The chat shows in **Chat**, like any other message.
    - The person is also saved in **Contacts**.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Do ice breakers use credits?">
    - Yes. Comentify uses credits when it sends your ice breakers to Meta and when it sends each reply.
    - See [Plans](/account/plans) for details.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
