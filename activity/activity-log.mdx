The **Activity Log** keeps a record of actions in your workspace.

* See who did something, what they did, and when.
* It records sign-ins and changes, such as creating a contact or deleting a flow.
* Nobody can edit or delete the log.

Use it when you need to know **who changed something** or **when it happened**.

## What the log records

| Type               | Examples                                                                 |
| ------------------ | ------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| **Sign-ins**       | Logged in, logged out, or a failed login attempt.                        |
| **Your account**   | Profile updated, password changed, or two-step sign-in turned on or off. |
| **Channels**       | A channel connected, disconnected, or deleted.                           |
| **Contacts**       | A contact created, updated, or deleted.                                  |
| **Chats**          | A new conversation started or a chat deleted.                            |
| **Automations**    | An automation deleted.                                                   |
| **Flows**          | A flow created, saved, turned on, turned off, or deleted.                |
| **Canned replies** | A canned reply created, updated, or deleted.                             |

## What you see in the list

| Column          | What it shows                                                                          |
| --------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Description** | A short description of what happened. For example: *Flow "Price guide" was turned on.* |
| **Event**       | The action type, such as created, updated, or deleted.                                 |
| **By**          | The team member who did it. **System** means Comentify did it automatically.           |
| **Subject**     | What the action was about, such as a contact or flow.                                  |
| **When**        | The date and time of the action.                                                       |

## Find an action

**Use the tabs:**

| Tab         | Shows                     |
| ----------- | ------------------------- |
| **All**     | All actions.              |
| **Created** | Things that were added.   |
| **Updated** | Things that were changed. |
| **Deleted** | Things that were removed. |

**Use the filters:**

1. Click **Filters**.
2. Choose an **Event**, a team member in **Performed By**, or dates in **From** and **To**.
3. Click **Clear filters** to remove the filters.

**Search:** type a word from the description, such as a contact's name.

Click **Refresh** to see the latest actions.

## Common questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Can I delete old entries?">
    - No. The Activity Log is a permanent record.
    - Nobody in your workspace can edit or delete it.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="What does System mean in the By column?">
    - Comentify did the action automatically, not a team member.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does it show the messages Comentify sent?">
    - No. This log shows actions in your workspace.
    - To see messages sent to Instagram and Facebook, use the [Meta Activity Log](/activity/meta-activity-log).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="I see a failed login attempt. What should I do?">
    - Someone entered the wrong password for an account in your workspace.
    - If it was not a team member, change the password.
    - Turn on two-step sign-in for extra security.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
