Statuses, sources and groups are labels you can use to organize your contacts.

| Label      | What it tells you                  | Examples                   | How many per contact |
| ---------- | ---------------------------------- | -------------------------- | -------------------- |
| **Status** | Where the person is with you.      | New, Interested, Paid      | One                  |
| **Source** | Where the person came from.        | Instagram, Website, Ads    | One                  |
| **Group**  | Which lists the person belongs to. | VIP, Wholesale, Newsletter | Many                 |

A new workspace has none of these. Create the ones you need.

## Where you use them

* **Contacts:** set a contact's status, source and groups, and filter contacts by them.
* **Contact defaults:** automatically give new contacts a status, source and groups.
* **Chat:** see the person's status and source in the contact details panel.
* **Flows:** the **Update the contact** node can change a person's status, source and groups.

<Tip>
  Set up your statuses and sources before connecting your first channel. This helps your first contacts get the right labels.
</Tip>

## Create a status

1. Click **Setup** in the left menu, then click **Statuses**.
2. Click **Add Status**.
3. Enter a **Name**, like "Interested".
4. Choose a **Color** for the status badge.
5. Click **Save**.

**Good statuses to start with:** New, Interested, Waiting for payment, Paid, Not interested.

## Create a source

1. Click **Setup** in the left menu, then click **Sources**.
2. Click **Add Source**.
3. Enter a **Name**, like "Instagram".
4. Click **Save**.

**Good sources to start with:** Instagram, Facebook, Website, Ads, Referral.

## Create a group

1. Click **Setup** in the left menu, then click **Groups**.
2. Click **Add Group**.
3. Enter a **Name**, like "VIP".
4. Click **Save**.

**Good groups to start with:** VIP, Wholesale, Newsletter, Event guests.

<Note>
  You can also create a status, source or group from a dropdown while editing a contact or setting up contact defaults.
</Note>

## Edit or delete

Click the menu (⋮) next to a status, source or group, then click **Edit** or **Delete**.

| When you delete a… | What happens to contacts                                         |
| ------------------ | ---------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Status**         | Contacts stay. Their status becomes empty and shows **Not set**. |
| **Source**         | Contacts stay. Their source becomes empty and shows **Not set**. |
| **Group**          | Contacts stay. The deleted group is removed from them.           |

You cannot undo a delete. Before deleting one, check the **Contact defaults** and flows that use it.

## Common questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="What is the difference between a status and a type?">
    - **Type** is fixed: **Lead**, **Guest** or **Customer**.
    - **Status** is custom: you can create any status you need.
    - You can use both. For example, Type **Lead** with Status "Interested".
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="What is the difference between a status and a group?">
    - A contact can have only one status at a time.
    - A contact can belong to many groups.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I sort contacts automatically?">
    - Yes. Use **Contact defaults** to give new contacts a status, source and groups.
    - Or use the **Update the contact** node in a flow to change them during a chat.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
