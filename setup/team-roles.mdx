You can add team members to your workspace, so others can help you in answer the messages.

- A **team member** is a person who signs in to your workspace.
- A **role** is a set of permissions, like "can reply in Chat" or "can delete contacts".
- You give each team member a role. The role decides what they can see and do.

## Create a role

A role is a set of permissions. For example, a "Support" role that can read and reply in **Chat**, but cannot delete anything.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Roles" icon="shield">
    Click **Setup** in the left menu, then click **Roles**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add a role" icon="plus">
    Click **Add Role**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Name the role" icon="tag">
    Type a name, like "Support" or "Sales".
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose permissions" icon="list-checks">
    Tick what this role can do. Permissions are grouped by feature, like **Chat**, **Contacts** or **Flows**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save" icon="save">
    Click **Save**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

### What the permissions mean

Most features have the same four permissions:

| Permission | What the person can do |
|---|---|
| **View** | See the page and its items. |
| **Create** | Add new items. |
| **Edit** | Change items. |
| **Delete** | Remove items. |

Some features have extra permissions:

| Feature | Extra permission | What it allows |
|---|---|---|
| **Chat** | View own | See only chats assigned to them. |
| **Chat** | Reply | Send messages from **Chat**. |
| **Chat** | Assign | Assign chats to team members. |
| **Contacts** | View own | See only contacts assigned to them. |
| **Contacts** | Export | Download the contact list. |
| **Channels** | Connect, Disconnect | Connect or disconnect Instagram and Facebook accounts. |
| **Settings** | View, Edit | See or change workspace settings. |
| **Billing** | View | See your plan, credits and invoices. |

<Tip>
  Use **View own** for sales or support staff. They see only their own chats and contacts, not everyone's.
</Tip>

### Example roles

| Role | Good permissions to give |
|---|---|
| **Support** | Chat: View, Reply. Contacts: View. |
| **Sales** | Chat: View own, Reply. Contacts: View own, Edit. |
| **Manager** | Everything except Billing and Settings. |

## Add a team member

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Team" icon="users">
    Click **Setup** in the left menu, then click **Team**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add a team member" icon="user-plus">
    Click **Add Team**. The **Create Team Member** panel opens.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Fill in their details" icon="id-card">
    On the **Team** tab, fill in:
    - **Name**
    - **Email:** they sign in with this email.
    - **Phone** (optional)
    - **Password** and **Confirm Password:** you choose their first password.

    Click **Next**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose their role" icon="shield">
    On the **Role** tab, choose one:
    - Turn on **Administrative Access** to give them full access to everything.
    - Or choose a **Role**. Need a new one? Click **Add Role** right there.

    Under **Permissions**, you can also change access for this one person:
    - Untick a permission to take it away from them only.
    - Tick an extra permission to give it to them only.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save and share" icon="send">
    - Click **Save**.
    - Tell the team member their email and password.
    - They sign in on the same sign-in page as you.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Check>
  You created a role, added a team member with that role, and shared their sign-in details.
</Check>

<Note>
  Comentify does not send an invite email. Share the email and password with your team member yourself. Ask them to change the password after they sign in.
</Note>

## Manage your team

| What you want to do | How to do it |
|---|---|
| Change a team member's role | Click the menu (⋮) next to them, then **Edit**. |
| Reset their password | Click **Edit** and type a new password. Leave it empty to keep the old one. |
| Remove a team member | Click the menu (⋮), then **Delete**. |
| See who uses a role | Open the role and look at **List of Users Using This Role**. |

<Note>
  You cannot edit your own account here. To change your own details, click your name at the bottom of the left menu, then **Account**.
</Note>

## Common questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="What is the difference between an administrator and a role?">
    - An administrator can do everything in the workspace.
    - A role gives only the permissions you tick.
    - The workspace owner is always an administrator.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Why can my team member not see a menu item?">
    - Their role does not include **View** for that feature.
    - Edit their role, or add the permission to them only.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="If I change a role, does it change for everyone?">
    - Yes. Everyone with that role gets the new permissions.
    - Personal changes you made to one team member stay in place.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Do team members use my credits?">
    - Yes. Messages your team sends from **Chat** use your workspace's credits.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can a team member see billing?">
    - Only if their role includes **Billing: View**, or they are an administrator.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
