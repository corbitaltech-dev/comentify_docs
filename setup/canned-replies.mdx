Save frequently used messages and reuse them in **Chat** whenever you need to reply quickly.

A **canned reply** is a saved message you can insert into a chat using a shortcut.

- **Save common replies:** Create messages for frequently asked questions, such as pricing, business hours, or product details.
- **Use shortcuts:** In **Chat**, type `/` followed by your shortcut, such as `/pricing`.
- **Insert replies quickly:** Comentify adds the saved message to the message box, ready for you to send.

**Example:** Type `/hours` in **Chat** to insert: "We are open from 9 AM to 5 PM, Monday to Friday."

Canned replies help you save time and respond to common questions more quickly.

## Create a canned reply

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Canned Replies" icon="message-square-text">
    Click **Setup** in the left menu, then click **Canned Replies**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add a reply" icon="plus">
    Click **Add Canned Reply**. The **New Canned Reply** panel opens.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Fill in the details" icon="square-pen">
    - **Title:** a name you will know, like "Pricing details".
    - **Shortcut:** the word you type after `/`, like `pricing`. Use only letters, numbers, `-` and `_`. Each shortcut must be different.
    - **Body:** the reply text. Type `@` to add the customer's name or other details.
    - **Attachment (optional):** drag a photo, video or audio file here, or click to choose one.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose who can use it" icon="users">
    - Leave **Shared with team** on, so your whole team can use it.
    - Turn it off to keep the reply only for yourself.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save" icon="save">
    Click **Save**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Check>
  You created a canned reply with a title, a shortcut and a body. It is ready to use in **Chat**.
</Check>

<Note>
  A canned reply needs a **Body** or an **Attachment**. If both are empty, you see **Add a body or an attachment.**
</Note>

## Use a canned reply in Chat

1. Open a chat in **Chat**.
2. In the message box, type `/`.
3. Keep typing the shortcut or title, like `pri`, to find the reply.
4. Click the reply. Comentify puts it in the message box.
5. Change anything you want, then click **Send message**.

You can also click the **Canned reply** button next to the message box.

## Manage your canned replies

| What you want to do | How to do it |
|---|---|
| Change a reply | Click the menu (⋮) next to it, then **Edit**. |
| Delete a reply | Click the menu (⋮), then **Delete**. |
| Delete many at once | Tick the replies, then click **Delete**. |

The list shows each reply's **Title**, **Shortcut**, **Body**, **Attachment** and whether it is **Shared with team** or **Private**.

## Common questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Can my team see my canned replies?">
    - Yes, if **Shared with team** is on.
    - If it is off, the reply is **Private**. Only you can see, edit and use it.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I use a canned reply in an automation?">
    - No. Canned replies are for **Chat** only.
    - In automations and flows, you write the message in the step itself.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Why does my shortcut not work?">
    - Check that you typed `/` first.
    - Check that the reply is shared, or that you created it yourself.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does sending a canned reply use credits?">
    - Yes. It is a normal message from **Chat**, so it uses credits like any other message.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I send a canned reply after 24 hours?">
    - No. Meta's 24-hour rule applies to every message from **Chat**.
    - You can reply only within 24 hours of the customer's last message.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
