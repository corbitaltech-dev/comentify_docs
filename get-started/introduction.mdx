Comentify is a social media automation platform that helps you automatically manage Instagram and Facebook comments and direct messages (DMs), engage with your audience, and reduce manual work.

- Someone **comments** on your post. Comentify can automatically reply to the comment or send a private message based on your automation settings.
- Someone **sends you a message**. Comentify can respond automatically using your configured rules.
- Someone **replies to your story** or **mentions your account**. You can configure supported automations to engage with these interactions.
- You configure your automation once, and Comentify **handles matching interactions automatically**.

## A simple example

Imagine you publish a new product on Instagram or Facebook and want to share its price with interested customers.

You write: *"Comment PRICE to get the product price in your DMs."*

1. A follower comments **PRICE** on your post.
2. Comentify detects the configured keyword.
3. Comentify automatically sends the follower a direct message containing the product price.
4. The follower is saved as a contact, allowing you to keep track of their interaction.

This process runs automatically whenever someone comments with the configured keyword and the automation conditions are met.

## How Comentify can help

- **Automate replies** to comments and messages to engage with your audience faster.
- **Share information automatically** when someone asks for a price, product link, or other details.
- **Manage conversations** from Instagram and Facebook in one place.
- **Build automated conversations** using questions, buttons, and step-by-step flows.
- **Save contacts** so you can keep track of people who interact with your accounts.

## How it works

<Steps>
  <Step title="Connect your account" icon="plug">
    Connect your Instagram Business or Creator account, or your Facebook Page, to Comentify.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create an automation" icon="zap">
    Choose the type of automation you want to use, configure keywords or triggers, and set up the reply or conversation flow.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Let Comentify handle the interaction" icon="circle-check">
    When someone interacts with your connected account and meets your automation conditions, Comentify responds automatically.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Words you will see in these docs

| Word | What it means |
|---|---|
| **Workspace** | Your space in Comentify where your connected accounts, contacts, and settings are managed. |
| **Channel** | An Instagram account or Facebook Page connected to Comentify. |
| **Automation** | A rule that triggers an automatic action, such as replying to a comment or sending a direct message. |
| **Keyword** | A word or phrase that triggers an automation, such as **PRICE** or **LINK**. |
| **Flow** | A visual canvas where you can create and arrange multiple steps in an automated conversation. |
| **Contact** | A person who interacts with your connected Instagram account or Facebook Page. |
| **Credit** | A unit used when Comentify performs a credit-based action. Your plan determines your available credits. |

## Where Comentify works

- **Instagram:** Supports Instagram Business and Creator accounts. Personal accounts are not supported.
- **Facebook:** Supports Facebook Pages and Messenger. Personal Facebook profiles are not supported.

<Tip>
  If your Instagram account is personal, you can switch it to a Business or Creator account for free. In the Instagram app, go to **Settings** → **Account type**.
</Tip>

## Common questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Do I need to be online for Comentify to reply?">
    - No. Comentify can reply automatically even when you are not online.
    - You can close your laptop or phone.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Do I give Comentify my Instagram password?">
    - No. You sign in through Meta's own login window.
    - Meta shows you the permissions you are giving.
    - You can disconnect your account from Comentify at any time.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Will Comentify post on my account?">
    - No. Comentify does not create posts or stories.
    - It only sends the replies and messages you set up.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I still answer messages myself?">
    - Yes. You can read and reply to messages from **Chat**.
    - When you reply yourself, Comentify pauses automatic replies in that chat.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can my team use Comentify with me?">
    - Yes. You can add team members and control what each person can see and do.
    - The number of team members depends on your plan.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="How much does Comentify cost?">
    - Comentify has different plans.
    - Each plan includes a different number of channels, automations, and credits.
    - See [Plans](/settings/billing) for details.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
