Create your Comentify account and set up your workspace to manage your connected Instagram and Facebook accounts, automations, conversations, and contacts in one place.

Your **workspace** is your dedicated space in Comentify where you manage your social media interactions and automation settings.

- **Connected accounts:** Connect your Instagram account or Facebook Page to manage interactions.
- **Automations and conversations:** Create automation rules, manage chats, and configure automated replies.
- **Contacts:** Keep track of people who interact with your connected social media accounts.
- **Team members:** Invite team members to collaborate in your workspace, if supported by your plan.

Your workspace is created when you sign up for Comentify. Once it's ready, you can connect your social media accounts and start setting up automations.

## Steps

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the sign-up page" icon="mouse-pointer-click">
    1. Go to [comentify.io](https://comentify.io).
    2. Click **Pricing**, choose a plan, and click **Get started**.

    The **Register** page opens.

    <Note>
      You can also sign up with **Google** or **Facebook**. You do not need to create a password when using one of these options.
    </Note>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Enter your personal details" icon="user">
    In **Personal Information**, enter:

    - **Full Name:** your name.
    - **Email:** your login email.
    - **Phone:** your country code and phone number.
    - **Country:** your country. This is optional.
    - **Plan:** If you did not select a plan before signing up, choose one here. Otherwise, this field is disabled.

    Click **Next**.

    {/* Screenshot: Register page, Personal Information tab */}
  </Step>

  <Step title="Enter your company details" icon="building-2">
    In **Company Information**, enter:

    - **Company Name:** your business name. This becomes your workspace name.
    - **Subdomain:** your workspace address. It is filled from your company name, but you can change it.
    - **Address:** your business address. This is optional.

    Click **Next**.

    {/* Screenshot: Register page, Company Information tab with the available message */}
  </Step>

  <Step title="Create your password" icon="lock">
    In **Security**, enter:

    - **Password:** create a password that follows the rules shown.
    - **Confirm Password:** enter the same password again.
    - Accept the **Terms & Conditions** and **Privacy Policy**.

    Click **Create Account**.

    {/* Screenshot: Register page, Security tab */}
  </Step>

  <Step title="Sign in to your workspace" icon="log-in">
    After your workspace is created:

    1. Enter your email and password.
    2. Click **Log in**.

    A mail sent to your email, open the verification email and click the link.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Check>
  Your workspace is created and you are signed in.
</Check>

## Rules for your workspace address

Your **Subdomain** is used in your workspace address.

- Use 3 to 63 characters.
- Use only lowercase letters, numbers, and hyphens (-).
- Do not start or end with a hyphen.
- Do not use two hyphens together.
- Do not use only numbers.
- Some common words, such as **admin**, **support**, and **help**, are not allowed.

For example, **acme-store** is valid. **Acme Store** is not valid because it contains capital letters and a space.

## Common questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Why is my phone number already used?">
    - Each phone number can be used for one workspace.
    - If you already created a workspace with this number, sign in to it.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Why is my subdomain not available?">
    - Another workspace may already use the name.
    - The name may not follow the [rules above](#rules-for-your-workspace-address).
    - Try a different name, such as **acme-shop**.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Should my team members sign up too?">
    - No. Only the owner needs to create the workspace.
    - Add team members from inside your workspace.
    - If a team member signs up separately, they will create a new workspace.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can I change my plan later?">
    - Yes. You can change your plan from your workspace settings.
    - See [Plans](/settings/billing) for details.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Why is my workspace waiting for approval?">
    - Some new workspaces need to be checked before they are opened.
    - You will receive an email when your workspace is approved.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="I did not receive the verification email.">
    - Check your spam or junk folder.
    - On the verification page, click **Resend verification email**.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
