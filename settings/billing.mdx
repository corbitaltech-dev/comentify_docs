Everything about your plan and payments is in **Settings** → **Billing**.

| Page         | What you do there                                 |
| ------------ | ------------------------------------------------- |
| **Plan**     | See your plan, end date, and upgrade options.     |
| **Usage**    | See how much of your plan you have used.          |
| **Credits**  | See your credits and buy more.                    |
| **History**  | See payments, plan changes, and credit purchases. |
| **Invoices** | View and download your invoices.                  |

For prices and plan details, see the [pricing section on comentify.io](https://comentify.io/#pricing).

## Your plan

Go to **Settings** → **Billing** → **Plan**.

You see:

* **Subscription:** Your plan name and status, such as **Active**.
* **Start date** and **End date:** When your plan starts and ends, and how many days are left.
* **Billing summary:** What you paid, including discounts and tax.
* **Renewal:** When the plan ends and the cost to renew.

### Upgrade your plan

1. Scroll to **Upgrade Options**.
2. Choose a bigger plan and click **Upgrade**.
3. Pay on the payment page. Learn more in [Choose and pay for your plan](/get-started/choose-plan).

Any unused amount from your current plan is deducted from the new price. You see this as **Plan credit**.

<Note>
  You can upgrade only while your plan is active. To move to a smaller or same-price plan, wait until your current plan is due to renew.
</Note>

If a plan shows **Usage over limit**, you are using more than that plan allows. Reduce your usage first, such as deleting unused automations, then switch plans.

### When your plan ends

* Comentify does not charge you automatically.
* We email you 7 days, 3 days, and 1 day before your plan ends.
* When the plan ends, you see **Renew Your Subscription**. Pay again to continue using your workspace.

## Usage and limits

Go to **Settings** → **Billing** → **Usage**.

Each item shows how much you used and the limit, such as "3 / 5". **Unlimited** means there is no limit.

Your plan can limit:

| Item               | What counts                                        |
| ------------------ | -------------------------------------------------- |
| **Channels**       | Connected accounts, including disconnected ones.   |
| **Automations**    | All automations, including turned-off ones.        |
| **Flows**          | All flows.                                         |
| **Contacts**       | All saved contacts.                                |
| **Conversations**  | All conversations started, including deleted ones. |
| **Team**           | Team members in your workspace.                    |
| **Roles**          | Roles you created.                                 |
| **Canned Replies** | Saved replies.                                     |
| **Chat History**   | How many days messages are kept.                   |
| **Credits**        | Credits included each month.                       |

You see **You are close to this limit.** when you reach 90% or more. At 100%, you see **You have reached this limit.**

<Warning>
  When you reach your contact or conversation limit, Comentify cannot save new contacts or start new conversations. It also cannot reply to them. Upgrade your plan to keep replying to new people.
</Warning>

## Credits

Credits are used when Comentify communicates with Instagram and Facebook for you.

### What uses credits

* **Sending a message:** Automatic replies, DMs, **Chat** replies, flow messages, and ice breakers.
* **Some Meta checks:** Such as reading a new comment or checking if someone follows you.

If Meta does not accept a message, no credits are used. Connecting accounts and syncing posts are also free.

### How credits work

* Your plan gives you credits every month.
* Plan credits refill on the 1st of each month. Unused credits do not carry over.
* You can buy extra credits. You can buy. Extra credits you buy and do not use carry forward to the next month.
* Comentify uses plan credits first, then bought credits.

### When credits run low

* At 70% used, you see **You're low on credits** at the top of every page.
* When you run out, you see **You're out of credits — automations have stopped sending.**
* Sending stops until you buy more credits or your plan credits refill on the 1st.

<Note>
  If your plan has unlimited credits, you do not need to buy more. You see **Unlimited credits on your current plan.**
</Note>

### Buy more credits

1. Go to **Settings** → **Billing** → **Credits**, or click **Buy Credits** on your dashboard.
2. In **Buy more credits**, use the slider to choose a pack.
3. Check the **Order summary** and click pay.
4. Choose a payment method:

   * **Razorpay:** Credits are added after payment.
   * **Offline Payment** (bank transfer): Enter your **Transaction ID** and click **Submit for review**. Credits are added after our team checks the payment.

## History and invoices

**History:** Go to **Settings** → **Billing** → **History**.

* See payments, plan changes, and credit purchases, newest first.
* Click **View invoice** to open an invoice.
* Click **Load older** to see older entries.

**Invoices:** Go to **Settings** → **Billing** → **Invoices**.

* See all your invoices in one place.
* Click the menu (⋮) next to an invoice to **View** or **Download** it.

## Common questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Does my plan renew by itself?">
    - No. We send reminder emails before it ends.
    - Pay again to renew your plan.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I move to a smaller plan?">
    - Not while your plan is active.
    - You can choose a smaller plan when it is due to renew.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Do unused plan credits carry over?">
    - No. Plan credits refill on the 1st of each month.
    - Extra credits you buy and do not use carry forward to the next month.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="My bank transfer for credits is waiting. What now?">
    - You see **Awaiting approval** on the **Credits** page.
    - Our team checks the payment and adds your credits.
    - If it shows **Payment rejected**, check your details and submit the payment again.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Where do I find my invoice?">
    - Go to **Settings** → **Billing** → **Invoices**.
    - Click **Download** next to the invoice.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
