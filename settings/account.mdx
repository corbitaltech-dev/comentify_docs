Your account page is for your personal account, not the whole workspace.

* Change your name, email, and photo.
* Change your password.
* Turn on two-step sign-in for extra security.
* See the devices signed in to your account.

Each team member has their own account page.

## Open your account

1. Click your name at the bottom of the left menu.
2. Click **Account**.

{/* Screenshot: Account page with profile card, sessions, two-factor card and profile form */}

## What you see

| Part                              | What it shows                                                                                                                             |
| --------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Your card**                     | Your photo, name, email, and badges like **Verified** and your plan. It also shows your **Phone**, **Joined** date, and **Last Updated**. |
| **Active Sessions**               | The devices signed in to your account. **This device** shows the device you are using now.                                                |
| **Two-Factor Authentication**     | Shows if two-step sign-in is on and lets you manage it.                                                                                   |
| **Profile** and **Password** tabs | Change your account details and password.                                                                                                 |
| **Recent Activity**               | Shows your **last 5 actions** in the workspace.                                                                                           |

## Change your photo

* Click your photo on your card and choose a new picture.
* Click **Remove photo** to use your initials again.

## Change your name or email

1. Open the **Profile** tab.
2. Change your **Name** or **Email Address**.
3. Click **Save**.

You see **Profile updated successfully.**

<Note>
  You use your email to sign in. If you change it, use the new email the next time you sign in.
</Note>

## Change your password

1. Open the **Password** tab.
2. Enter your **Current Password**.
3. Enter your **New Password** and enter it again to confirm.
4. Click **Save**.

You see **Password updated successfully.**

<Tip>
  Forgot your password? Sign out and click **Forgot password?** on the sign-in page. We will email you a link to set a new password.
</Tip>

## Turn on two-step sign-in

Two-step sign-in asks for a code from your phone when you sign in. This adds extra security to your account.

You need an authenticator app on your phone, such as Google Authenticator or Microsoft Authenticator.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Start" icon="shield">
    In **Two-Factor Authentication**, click **Enable 2FA**. Enter your password to confirm.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Scan the QR code" icon="scan-line">
    Open your authenticator app and scan the QR code on the screen. You can also enter the setup key manually.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter the code" icon="key-round">
    Enter the 6-digit code from your app and confirm it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save your recovery codes" icon="file-lock">
    - Click **View recovery codes**.
    - Save them somewhere safe, such as a password manager.
    - Use a recovery code if you lose your phone.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Check>
  Two-step sign-in is now **Enabled**. Next time you sign in, Comentify will ask for a code from your authenticator app.
</Check>

### Manage two-step sign-in

| What you want to do     | How to do it                                                  |
| ----------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------- |
| See your recovery codes | Click **View recovery codes**.                                |
| Get new recovery codes  | Click **Regenerate codes**. Your old codes will stop working. |
| Turn it off             | Click **Disable 2FA** and enter your password.                |

Each recovery code can be used only once.

### Sign in with two-step sign-in

1. Enter your email and password as usual.
2. Enter the 6-digit code from your authenticator app.
3. If you lost your phone, click **Use a recovery code** and enter one of your saved codes.

## Common questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="I see a device in Active Sessions that I do not know. What do I do?">
    - Change your password right away.
    - Turn on two-step sign-in.
    - Check the **Activity Log** for actions you did not make.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="I lost my phone and my recovery codes. How do I get in?">
    - Ask your workspace owner or our support team for help.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does changing my account change the workspace?">
    - No. Your account page changes only your personal details.
    - Workspace settings are in **Settings**. Learn more in [Workspace settings](/settings/workspace).
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
