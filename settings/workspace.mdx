Workspace settings control how Comentify works for your team.

* **General:** Set your time zone, country, and date format.
* **Miscellaneous:** Choose how many rows show in lists.
* **Contact Defaults:** Set the details for new contacts.
* **Theme:** Change the colors and style of the app.

## Open settings

In the left menu, click **Settings**. You will see two tabs:

* **Core Platform:** **General**, **Miscellaneous**, and **Contact Defaults**.
* **Billing:** Your plan, usage, credits, and invoices. Learn more in [Plan, credits & billing](/settings/billing).

## General

Set your time zone and date format.

| Setting         | What it does                                                                                                           |
| --------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Timezone**    | Sets the time zone used for dates and times in your workspace. Search for your city or time zone.                      |
| **Country**     | Sets your workspace country.                                                                                           |
| **Date Format** | Choose how dates are shown: **YYYY-MM-DD**, **DD/MM/YYYY**, **MM/DD/YYYY**, or a long format like "February 13, 2026". |
| **Time Format** | Choose **24 hour** (like 14:30) or **12 hour (AM/PM)** (like 2:30 PM).                                                 |

Click **Save Changes** when you are done.

<Tip>
  Set your time zone first. This keeps the times in **Chat**, **Contacts**, and logs correct.
</Tip>

## Miscellaneous

**Tables Pagination Limit:** Sets how many rows appear on each page in lists like contacts and automations.

## Contact Defaults

Contact defaults are the details added when Comentify creates a new contact.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Contact Defaults" icon="user-plus">
    Go to **Settings** → **Core Platform** → **Contact Defaults**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose the defaults" icon="list-checks">
    - **Type:** **Lead**, **Guest**, or **Customer**. The default is **Guest**.
    - **Status:** The status given to new contacts.
    - **Source:** Where the contact came from, like "Instagram".
    - **Assign To:** The team member assigned to the contact. Leave it as **Unassigned** if needed.
    - **Groups:** The groups in which new contacts are added to.
    
    You can create a new status, source, or group from the dropdown.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save" icon="save">
    Click save. You will see **Global defaults saved successfully.**
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  Defaults apply only when a contact is created. They do not change existing contacts.
</Note>

### Different defaults for one channel

You can set different defaults for contacts from a specific channel. For example, Facebook contacts can have Source set to "Facebook".

1. Go to **Channels**.
2. Click the menu (⋮) next to the channel, then **Contact Defaults**.
3. Turn on **Override global defaults**.
4. Choose the defaults for this channel and save.

When the switch is off, the channel uses the main defaults.

## Common questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Why do times in Chat look wrong?">
    - Your **Timezone** may be incorrect.
    - Go to **Settings** → **General** and choose the correct time zone.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="I changed Contact Defaults. Why did old contacts not change?">
    - Defaults apply only to new contacts.
    - To change existing contacts, edit them in **Contacts**.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
