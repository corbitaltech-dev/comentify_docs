The **Dashboard** gives you a quick overview of your workspace activity and usage.

- Check your usage numbers and activity at a glance.
- Monitor messages, contacts, conversations, and active automations.
- Check your connected channels, plan, and available credits.
- Use the dashboard cards to open the related settings or pages.

## Other dashboard cards

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Credits" icon="coins">
    - **Credits available:** Credits you have left.
    - **Plan:** Credits included with your plan per month.
    - **Purchased:** Extra credits you have bought.
    - **Resets:** Date when your plan credits are reset.
    - Click **Buy Credits** to purchase more.

    Learn more: [Plans and credits](/account/plans)
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Messages" icon="chart-column">
    - See the number of messages **Sent** and **Received**.
    - Choose a time range such as **Today** or **Last 7 days**.

    Learn more: [How Chat works](/inbox/shared-inbox)
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Send Volume by Category" icon="chart-no-axes-column">
    - See which type of message Comentify sends most.
    - For example, compare automatic DMs and comment replies.

    Learn more: [Automatic DMs](/automation/dm-automation)
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Contact Engagement" icon="chart-pie">
    - See your contacts grouped as **Lead**, **Guest**, or **Customer**.
    - A **Lead** may become a customer.
    - A **Customer** has already purchased.
    - A **Guest** is any other contact.

    Learn more: [How contacts work](/inbox/contacts)
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Your Plan" icon="credit-card">
    - See your current plan and remaining days.
    - Check how much of your plan you have used.
    - Click **Manage Plan** to view or change your plan.

    Learn more: [Choose and pay for your plan](/choose-plan)
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Channel Health" icon="heart-pulse">
    - See the status of your connected Instagram accounts and Facebook Pages.
    - If a channel needs attention, you can reconnect it.
    - If you see **No channels connected yet**, connect your first account.

    Learn more: [Connect your accounts](/channels/connect-accounts)
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Recent Activity" icon="history">
    - See the latest messages sent by Comentify.
    - **Nothing sent yet** means no automation has sent a message yet.

    Learn more: [Turn on your first automation](/quickstart)
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Waiting on Reply" icon="message-circle-more">
    - See people who sent you a message and are waiting for your reply.
    - Open **Chat** to reply.

    Learn more: [How Chat works](/inbox/shared-inbox)
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Quick Access" icon="mouse-pointer-click">
    Use the shortcuts to quickly open **Chat**, **Automations**, **Contacts**, or **Channels**.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Left menu

Use the left menu to move around your workspace.

| Menu item | What you can do | Learn more |
|---|---|---|
| **Dashboard** | View your main workspace numbers. | You are here |
| **Channels** | Connect Instagram accounts and Facebook Pages. | [Connect your accounts](/channels/connect-accounts) |
| **Chat** | Read and reply to messages. | [How Chat works](/inbox/shared-inbox) |
| **Contacts** | View people who commented or messaged you. | [How contacts work](/inbox/contacts) |
| **Flows** | Build conversations with multiple steps. | [How flows work](/automation/workflows) |
| **Automations** | Create automatic replies to comments and messages. | [How automations work](/automation/comment-replies) |
| **Activity Log** | View changes made in your workspace. | |
| **Settings** | Manage your workspace, plan, and credits. | [Settings](/account/settings) |
| **Setup** | Manage team members, roles, statuses, sources, groups, and canned replies. | [Team members](/team/team-access) |

<Tip>
  Click your name at the bottom of the left menu, then click **Account** to change your name, password, or two-step sign-in settings.
</Tip>
