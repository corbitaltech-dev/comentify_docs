With Comentify, you can automate your replies and stay connected with your audience.

- Someone **comments** on your post. Comentify can reply to them.
- Someone **sends you a message**. Comentify can answer it.
- You set up the reply once, and Comentify **sends it automatically**.

This saves your time and helps you respond to people without waiting.

## A simple example

You post a new product on Instagram and write: "Comment **price** to get the price in your DMs."

1. A follower comments **price**.
2. Comentify detects the keyword **price**.
3. Comentify sends them a direct message (DM) with the price.
4. The follower is saved as a contact.

This can happen automatically whenever someone comments **price**.

## How Comentify Can Help

- **Automate replies** to common comments and messages.
- **Share information automatically** when someone asks for a price, link, or other details.
- **Manage conversations** from Instagram and Facebook in one place.
- **Save contacts** so you can keep track of people who interact with your accounts.

## Words you will see in these docs

| Word | What it means |
|---|---|
| **Workspace** | Your space in Comentify where your accounts, contacts, and settings are managed. |
| **Channel** | An Instagram account or Facebook Page connected to Comentify. |
| **Automation** | A rule that sends a reply automatically, such as sending the price when someone comments **price**. |
| **Keyword** | A word that starts an automation, such as **price** or **link**. |
| **Flow** |  A visual canvas where you can create and arrange multiple steps for a conversation. |
| **Contact** | A person who comments on your post or sends you a message. |
| **Credit** | A unit used when Comentify sends a message for you. Your plan includes a monthly number of credits. |

## Where Comentify works

- **Instagram:** Business and Creator accounts. Personal accounts are not supported.
- **Facebook:** Facebook Pages and Messenger. Personal Facebook profiles are not supported.

<Tip>
  If your Instagram account is personal, you can switch it to a Business or Creator account for free. In the Instagram app, go to **Settings** → **Account type**.
</Tip>

## Common questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Do I need to be online for Comentify to reply?">
    - No. Comentify can reply automatically even when you are not online.
    - You can close your laptop or phone.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Do I give Comentify my Instagram password?">
    - No. You sign in through Meta's own login window.
    - Meta shows you the permissions you are giving.
    - You can disconnect your account from Comentify at any time.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Will Comentify post on my account?">
    - No. Comentify does not create posts or stories.
    - It only sends the replies and messages you set up.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I still answer messages myself?">
    - Yes. You can read and reply to messages from **Chat**.
    - When you reply yourself, Comentify pauses automatic replies in that chat.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can my team use Comentify with me?">
    - Yes. You can add team members and control what each person can see and do.
    - The number of team members depends on your plan.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="How much does Comentify cost?">
    - Comentify has different plans.
    - Each plan includes a different number of channels, automations, and credits.
    - See [Plans](/account/plans) for details.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
