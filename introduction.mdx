## What Comentify Does

When someone comments on your Instagram post or Facebook Page, Comentify can automatically:

- Reply publicly to the comment
- Send that person a private direct message
- Capture their details as a lead
- Route the conversation to the right team member in your shared inbox

You configure this behavior through **Automations** and **Workflows** — no coding required.

## Supported Platforms

Comentify currently works with:

- **Instagram** — Professional accounts (Business or Creator)
- **Facebook** — Pages (personal profiles are not supported)

Connections are made through Meta's official authorization flow, so your credentials are never stored by Comentify.
