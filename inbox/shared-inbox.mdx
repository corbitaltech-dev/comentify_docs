Comentify's shared inbox brings every Instagram DM and Facebook Page message into a single, collaborative workspace so your team can respond faster, stay coordinated, and never let a conversation slip through the cracks.

## What Is the Shared Inbox?

The shared inbox is a central hub where every team member can see, reply to, and manage incoming social conversations in real time. Instead of logging into separate Instagram or Facebook accounts, your whole team works from one unified view — making it ideal for customer support teams, community managers, and social media teams handling high message volumes.

<Note>
  The shared inbox supports both Instagram Direct Messages and Facebook Page messages. All conversations from connected accounts appear in a single, unified list.
</Note>

### Conversation List

The left panel displays all conversations sorted by most recent activity. Each row shows the contact's name, profile picture, a message preview, and the conversation's current status. Use the top navigation tabs to switch between **All**, **Open**, **Pending**, and **Resolved** conversations.

### Conversation View

Selecting a conversation opens the full message thread in the center panel. You can read the entire history of the exchange, see the contact's platform and username, and review any tags or notes attached to their contact record.

## Sending Replies

### Typing a Reply

Click the reply composer at the bottom of the conversation view, type your message, and press **Send** or hit `Enter` to deliver it. Your reply is sent instantly through the connected Instagram or Facebook account.

### Using Saved Responses

Speed up common replies with saved responses (also called quick replies). To insert one:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the composer">
    Click inside the reply text field in an open conversation.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open saved responses">
    Click the **Saved Responses** icon (lightning bolt) in the composer toolbar.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select a response">
    Browse or search your saved responses and click one to insert it into the composer.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Edit and send">
    Personalize the text if needed, then press **Send**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Assigning Conversations

Assign any conversation to a specific team member to make ownership clear and prevent duplicate replies.

<Tip>
  Always assign conversations before replying. This prevents two team members from sending separate responses to the same message at the same time.
</Tip>

To assign a conversation:

1. Open the conversation in the inbox.
2. Click the **Assign** button in the top-right panel.
3. Select a team member from the dropdown list.
4. The conversation now appears in that team member's **Assigned to Me** view.

You can reassign a conversation at any time by repeating these steps. To learn how to add more team members to your workspace, see [Team Access](/team/team-access).

## Conversation Statuses

Every conversation has one of three statuses that reflect where it is in your workflow:

<Accordion title="Open">
  The conversation is active and requires attention. New incoming messages automatically set a conversation to **Open**.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Pending">
  You have replied and are waiting for a response from the contact. Mark a conversation as **Pending** to signal that no immediate action is needed from your team.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Resolved">
  The conversation is complete. Mark a conversation as **Resolved** to remove it from the active queue. Resolved conversations remain searchable in your history.
</Accordion>

To change a conversation's status, click the status badge in the top-right corner of the conversation view and select the new status from the dropdown.

## Filtering and Searching Conversations

Use the search bar at the top of the conversation list to find a specific message or contact by keyword, username, or phrase. To narrow results further, apply filters:

- **Status** — show only Open, Pending, or Resolved conversations
- **Assignee** — show conversations assigned to a specific team member or unassigned
- **Platform** — show only Instagram or only Facebook messages
- **Tag** — show contacts with a specific tag applied to their record

Combine multiple filters to build a precise view of your queue.
