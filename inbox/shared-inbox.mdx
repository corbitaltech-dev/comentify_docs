**Chat** is where you read and reply to your messages.

- Instagram and Facebook messages appear in one place.
- You and your team can reply from the same screen.
- See which chats need a reply.

When you reply yourself, automation pauses replies in that chat. This prevents both you and automation from replying at the same time.

## The Chat screen

The Chat screen has three parts.

| Part | What you see |
|---|---|
| **Left:** the chat list | All conversations, with the newest message first. |
| **Middle:** the open chat | Messages and the reply box. |
| **Right:** contact details | Contact information and private team notes. Click **Open contact details** to show it. |

### Tabs in the chat list

| Tab | What it shows |
|---|---|
| **All** | All conversations. |
| **Unread** | Conversations with unread messages. |
| **Mine** | Conversations assigned to you. |
| **Unassigned** | Conversations not assigned to any team member. |
| **Needs you** | People waiting for a reply. |

### Signs in the chat list

- A red **Needs you** badge means the person is waiting for a reply.
- A lock icon means the 24-hour reply window is closed.
- **Paused** means automatic replies are paused in the chat.
- A number shows unread messages.

### Find a chat

- Enter a name or username in **Search people**.
- Click **Filter conversations** to filter by status, team member, type, or source.

## Reply to a message

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the chat" icon="mouse-pointer-click">
    Click a conversation in the list.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Type your reply" icon="square-pen">
    - Type your message in the box.
    - Type `/` to use a saved reply.
    - Type `@` to add details such as the person's name, for example `{{first_name}}`.
    - Click **Emoji** to add an emoji.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Add a photo, video or audio (optional)" icon="paperclip">
    - Click **Attach media**.
    - Choose **Photo**, **Video**, or **Audio**.
    - Select the file from your computer.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Send" icon="send">
    Click **Send message**.

    Comentify pauses automatic replies in this chat after you send a message.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Check>
  Your message is sent and automatic replies are paused in this chat.
</Check>

### Reply to one specific message

You can reply directly to a specific message.

1. Click (⋮) on the message.
2. Click **Reply**.
3. Type your answer and send it.

<Note>
  This works only for channels connected with **Connect with Facebook**. Meta does not allow it for **Connect with Instagram**. Learn more in [Connect your accounts](/channels/connect-accounts#connect-with-instagram-or-facebook).
</Note>

## When a person needs you

Comentify marks a chat as **Needs you** when:

- The person sent a message and no automation or flow replied.
- The person messaged while a team member was handling the chat.

The chat shows **Needs you · waiting for a reply from your team**.

To clear it:

- Reply to the person, or
- Click **Mark as handled** if no reply is needed.

<Tip>
  Check the **Needs you** tab regularly. The Dashboard also shows when chats are waiting for your team.
</Tip>

## Why automatic replies pause when you reply

When a team member sends a message, automations pauses replies in that chat for 24 hours. This prevents automations from replying at the same time.

At the top of the chat, you see **Automation paused · a team member replied · resumes in** with the remaining time.

- **What pauses:** Automatic DM replies and flows in this chat.
- **What keeps working:** Comment automations can still reply when the person comments on a post.
- **To start again early:** Click **Resume**.

## The 24-hour reply window

Meta allows businesses to reply within 24 hours of a person's last message.

- Each new message starts a new 24-hour window.
- The chat shows the remaining time or **Window open**.
- When the time ends, you see **Window expired**.
- You cannot reply until the person messages again.

<Note>
  This is a Meta rule, not a Comentify rule.
</Note>

## Give a chat to a team member

1. Open the chat.
2. Click the assign button at the top. It shows **Unassigned** or a team member's name.
3. In **Assign conversation**, select one or more team members.
4. Click **Apply**.

The first selected person becomes the main owner of the chat. The chat appears in their **Mine** tab.

To assign new chats automatically, set **Assign To** in **Contact Defaults**. Learn more in [Contacts](/inbox/contacts#before-you-start).

## Contact details

Click **Open contact details** at the top of a chat. A panel opens on the right with two tabs: **Details** and **Notes**.

At the top, you see the person's name, username and type, like **Guest**, **Lead** or **Customer**.

{/* Screenshot: Contact details panel with the Details tab open */}

The **Details** tab has three parts.

### Contact information

| Field | What it shows |
|---|---|
| **Email** | The person's email, if they shared it. Otherwise it says **Not provided**. |
| **Phone** | The person's phone number, if you have it. Otherwise it says **Not provided**. |
| **Channel** | Where they contact to you: **Instagram** or **Facebook**. |

### Relationship

| Field | What it shows |
|---|---|
| **Lifecycle** | How recently the person was active. **Active** means the last 30 days. **Cooling** means 30 to 90 days ago. **Dormant** means over 90 days ago. |
| **Status** | Where the person is with you. It says **Not set** until you choose one. |
| **Source** | Where the person came from. It says **Not set** until you choose one. |
| **Audience** | **Follower** if they follow your Instagram account, or **Not a follower** if they do not. Instagram only. |
| **Profile** | **Verified** if their Instagram account has a blue badge, or **Not verified** if it does not. Instagram only. |

### Activity

| Field | What it shows |
|---|---|
| **Messages** | How many messages the person sent you. |
| **Comments** | How many comments the person left on your posts. |
| **First seen** | When the person contact you first time. |
| **Last activity** | When the person send last message or commented. |

<Tip>
  To change the email, phone, status or source, edit the person in **Contacts**. Learn more in [Contacts](/inbox/contacts#add-or-edit-a-contact).
</Tip>

## Private notes

Private notes help your team share information about a person. The person cannot see them.

1. Click **Open contact details**.
2. Open the **Notes** tab.
3. Enter your note in **Write an internal note...** and click **Add note**.

Notes are saved with the contact and also appear in **Contacts**.

## Search inside a chat

- Click **Search messages** at the top of the chat.
- Enter a word or phrase.
- Press Enter for older matches and Shift+Enter for newer matches.

## Clear or delete a chat

Click **Conversation actions** at the top of the chat.

| Option | What happens |
|---|---|
| **Clear chat** | Removes messages and notes. The contact and conversation stay, so new messages can still arrive. |
| **Delete chat** | Permanently removes the conversation, messages, and notes. |

You cannot undo either action.

## Saved replies

Saved replies, called **canned replies** in the app, are messages you can reuse.

- Type `/` in the reply box to find a saved reply.
- Keep typing to search, then select one.
- A saved reply can include text, a photo, video, or audio.

Create saved replies in **Setup** → **Canned Replies**.

## Common questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Why can I not type a reply?">
    - The 24-hour window may be closed. Wait for the person to message again.
    - The channel may have a problem. Check its status in **Channels**.
    - Your role may not allow you to reply. Ask your workspace owner.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Why do I not see Reply on a message?">
    - The channel was connected with **Connect with Instagram**.
    - Meta allows replies to specific messages only for **Connect with Facebook**.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Why can my team member see only some chats?">
    - Their role may limit which chats they can see.
    - Change their access in **Setup** → **Roles**.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Do my replies from Chat use credits?">
    - Yes. Messages sent by Comentify to Meta use credits.
    - See [Plans](/account/plans) for details.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="How long does Comentify keep my messages?">
    - It depends on your plan.
    - Older messages are removed after the number of days allowed by your plan.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What happens when I reach my conversation limit?">
    - Your plan has a limit on new conversations.
    - When you reach the limit, Comentify cannot start new conversations with new people.
    - Existing conversations continue to work.
    - Upgrade your plan to start new conversations.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can I answer Instagram comments from Chat?">
    - No. **Chat** is for direct messages.
    - Use automations to reply to comments.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
