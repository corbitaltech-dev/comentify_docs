A **contact** is a person who comments on your post, sends you a message, or mentions you.

- Comentify automatically creates a contact when someone interacts with you.
- This happens even if you do not have an automation turned on.
- All contacts are shown in **Contacts**.

You can export your contacts at any time.

## Before you start

Set these options before people become contacts. New contacts use these settings when they are created.

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Contact defaults" icon="user-plus">
    - Set the default **Type**, **Status**, and **Source** for new contacts.
    - You can also assign new contacts to a team member.
    - Go to **Settings** → **Contact Defaults** to set them.
    - To use different defaults for a channel, go to **Channels**, click the menu (⋮), and select **Contact Defaults**.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Statuses, sources and groups" icon="list">
    - **Status:** Shows where the person is, such as "New" or "Interested".
    - **Source:** Shows where the person came from, such as "Instagram" or "Website".
    - **Group:** Organizes contacts, such as "VIP". A contact can belong to multiple groups.
    - Create them in **Setup** → **Statuses**, **Sources**, and **Groups**.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## How contacts are created

1. Someone comments on your post, sends you a DM, or mentions you.
2. Comentify checks if they already exist as a contact.
3. If not, Comentify creates a contact using your default settings.
4. Their name and profile picture are added from Instagram or Facebook.

<Note>
  The same person on Instagram and Facebook is saved as **two contacts**. Meta gives each person a different ID on each platform.
</Note>

## What you see in the list

| Column | What it means |
|---|---|
| **Name** | The person's name and username. The icon shows Instagram or Facebook. |
| **Type** | **Lead** may buy from you. **Customer** has bought. **Guest** is anyone else. |
| **Status** | Shows where the person is with you. |
| **Source** | Shows where the person came from. |
| **Assignee** | The team member assigned to the person. |
| **Active** | Turns automatic replies on or off for the person. |

You can show more columns, such as **Comments**, **DMs**, and **Created**, from the column menu.

## Stop automatic replies for one person

Each contact has an **Active** switch.

- **On:** Automations and flows can reply to the person.
- **Off:** Automations and flows do not reply to the person. You can still reply in **Chat**.

To turn it off:

1. Go to **Contacts**.
2. Turn off the **Active** switch next to the person.

You will see **Contact deactivated — automations and flows will not reply**.

Turn the switch on again to allow automatic replies.

<Tip>
  When a deactivated contact sends you a message, Comentify marks them **Needs you** so your team knows to reply.
</Tip>

## What "Needs you" means

A red **Needs you** badge means the person is waiting for a reply from your team.

It appears when:

- The person sends a message and no automation or flow replies.
- A flow hands the conversation over to a team member.

The badge disappears when a team member replies in **Chat** or clicks **Mark as handled**.

You may also see **Can reply**. This means the person contacted you within the last 24 hours and you can still send them a message.

## View a contact

1. Go to **Contacts**.
2. Click the person's name.

A panel opens with two tabs:

- **Contact Details:** Type, status, source, assignee, groups, and last active time.
- **Notes:** Private notes about the person.

To add a note, enter it in **Add a note...** and click **Add Note**. Notes also appear in **Chat**.

## Add or edit a contact

**To add a contact:**
1. Click **Add Contact**.
2. Enter the contact details.
3. Click **Save**.

**To edit a contact:**
1. Click the menu (⋮) next to the person.
2. Click **Edit**.
3. Update the details and save.

<Warning>
  Manually added contacts cannot receive Instagram or Facebook messages from Comentify until they interact with you. Meta requires the person to message you first. You can still use manually added contacts to keep records such as email addresses or phone numbers.
</Warning>

## Give a contact to a team member

- Open **Edit** on the contact.
- Choose a team member in **Assigned To**.
- New chats with this person go to that team member in **Chat**.

To assign all new contacts to one team member, set **Assign To** in **Contact Defaults**.

## Find contacts with filters

Click **Filters** above the list. You will see two tabs:

- **Filters:** Use common filters such as type, status, source, assignee, or platform.
- **Saved Filters:** Use filters saved by you or your team.

### Build an advanced filter

1. Open **Filters** and click **New Filter**.
2. Click **Add rule** and choose a filter.
3. Choose **All conditions** or **Any condition**.
4. Click **Apply**.

| Rule | Finds people who… |
|---|---|
| **Needs you** | Are waiting for a team reply. |
| **Can be messaged now** | Contacted you in the last 24 hours. |
| **Follows you** | Follow your Instagram account. |
| **Engagement** | Are **Active**, **Cooling**, or **Dormant** based on recent activity. |
| **Last active** | Were active around a selected date. |
| **Has email** | Have provided an email address. |

### Save a filter

1. Turn on **Save filter** and enter a name.
2. Turn on **Share with other team members?** if needed.
3. Turn on **Mark as default** if you want to use it automatically.
4. Click **Apply and Save**.

## Export your contacts

1. Search or filter the list if you want to export specific contacts.
2. Click **Export**.

Comentify downloads a CSV file that you can open in Excel or Google Sheets.

The file includes the contact's name, username, phone, email, platform, type, status, source, assignee, and date added.

## Delete a contact

1. Click the menu (⋮) next to the person.
2. Click **Delete**.
3. Confirm the deletion.

You cannot undo this.

## Common questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Why do I see two contacts for one person?">
    - The person contacted you on both Instagram and Facebook.
    - Meta uses a different ID for the person on each platform, so Comentify creates two contacts.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can I import contacts from a file?">
    - Not yet. Comentify creates contacts when people interact with you.
    - You can add contacts one at a time with **Add Contact**.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What happens when I reach my contact limit?">
    - Your plan has a contact limit.
    - When you reach the limit, Comentify cannot save new contacts or reply to new people.
    - Existing contacts continue to work.
    - Upgrade your plan or delete contacts you no longer need.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Why can my team member see only some contacts?">
    - Their role may limit which contacts they can see.
    - Change their access in **Setup** → **Roles**.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Why is the name different from Instagram?">
    - Comentify gets the name and picture from Instagram or Facebook.
    - If you change the name manually, Comentify keeps your change.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="If I delete a channel, do I lose my contacts?">
    - No. Your contacts stay in **Contacts**.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
