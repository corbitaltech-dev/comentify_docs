Every person who sends you a DM, replies to your content, or gets captured through a lead automation is automatically saved as a contact in Comentify — giving your team a complete, searchable record of your audience in one place.

## What Are Contacts?

Contacts are individual profiles Comentify creates for every person who engages with your connected Instagram or Facebook accounts. A contact record is generated automatically when someone:

- Sends you an Instagram Direct Message or Facebook Page message
- Interacts with content that triggers one of your automations
- Submits their information through a [lead capture workflow](/inbox/lead-capture)

You don't need to import or manually create contacts. Comentify builds and updates them in the background as engagement happens.

<Note>
  Contact details such as name, profile picture, and username are read directly from Meta's platform and reflect the person's public profile information. Comentify cannot modify these fields.
</Note>

### Conversation History

The conversation history panel on a contact record shows every DM thread associated with that person, listed in chronological order. Click any conversation to open it in the shared inbox.

## Browsing and Searching Contacts

To access your contacts, navigate to **Inbox → Contacts** in the left sidebar. The contacts list displays all records sorted by most recent activity.

Use the **search bar** at the top of the list to find a contact by name, username, or platform handle. To filter results, use the available options:

- **Platform** — filter by Instagram or Facebook
- **Tag** — show only contacts with a specific tag
- **Date added** — sort by when the contact was first created

## Adding Tags to Contacts

Tags let you categorize contacts so you can segment your audience and filter conversations quickly.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the contact record">
    Navigate to **Contacts** and click a contact's name to open their record.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Add Tag">
    In the **Tags** section of the contact panel, click **+ Add Tag**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose or create a tag">
    Select an existing tag from the dropdown or type a new tag name and press `Enter` to create it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    Tags are saved automatically. The contact will now appear when you filter by that tag.
  </Step>
</Steps>

You can add multiple tags to a single contact. To remove a tag, click the **×** next to it in the contact record.

## Adding Notes to a Contact Record

Notes are private, internal annotations only your team can see. Use them to add context about a contact — for example, flagging a VIP customer or recording the outcome of a support interaction.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the contact record">
    Click on a contact in the **Contacts** list to open their profile.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Go to the Notes section">
    Scroll to the **Notes** panel on the right-hand side of the contact record.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Write your note">
    Click **+ Add Note**, type your text, and click **Save**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Notes are timestamped and attributed to the team member who wrote them. You can edit or delete your own notes at any time.

## Exporting Contacts

You can export your contacts list as a CSV file to use in external tools such as CRMs or email marketing platforms.

<Note>
  Contact export may be limited to certain plans. If the export option is not visible in your workspace, check your current plan in **Settings → Billing**.
</Note>

To export contacts:

1. Navigate to **Contacts** and apply any filters you want to limit the export scope.
2. Click the **Export** button in the top-right corner.
3. Choose **Export as CSV** and confirm.
4. The file downloads to your device with all visible contact fields included.
