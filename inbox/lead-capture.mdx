Lead capture turns everyday social engagement into a pipeline of qualified contacts by automatically asking followers for their information through a friendly DM conversation — no forms, no landing pages, and no manual follow-up required.

## What Lead Capture Does

When someone interacts with your Instagram or Facebook content — by commenting on a post, reacting to a story, or sending a keyword — Comentify can trigger a DM conversation that collects structured information from them. The data your contact provides is saved directly to their record in [Contacts](/inbox/contacts), making it immediately available to your team.

Lead capture collects any combination of the following fields:

- **Name** — the person's first and/or last name
- **Email address** — for follow-up email marketing or CRM syncing
- **Phone number** — for SMS outreach or sales follow-up

All collected data is stored securely on the contact's record and is visible to your entire team in the shared inbox.

## How It Works

Lead capture is an **action** inside a Comentify Workflow. You define the trigger (for example, a comment containing a specific keyword), and then add a **Capture Lead** action to the workflow. When the trigger fires, Comentify sends a sequence of DMs to the contact, prompting them for each piece of information you've chosen to collect. Their replies are parsed automatically and saved to their contact record.

Because the data collection happens inside a DM conversation, the experience feels natural to the contact — more like chatting than filling out a form.

## Add Lead Capture to a Workflow

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the workflow builder">
    Navigate to **Automation → Workflows** and open an existing workflow, or click **+ New Workflow** to create one. Make sure your workflow has at least one trigger configured before adding actions.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add a Capture Lead action">
    In the workflow canvas, click the **+** button to add a new action step. From the action library, select **Capture Lead**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose the fields to collect">
    In the action configuration panel, toggle on the fields you want to collect: **Name**, **Email**, and/or **Phone Number**. Only enable the fields you genuinely need.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Customize the messages">
    For each enabled field, edit the DM text Comentify sends to ask for that information. Personalize the message to match your brand voice. You can use the `{{first_name}}` variable to address the contact by name if it's already known.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save and activate the workflow">
    Click **Save** in the top-right corner of the builder, then toggle the workflow to **Active**. The lead capture sequence will now run automatically whenever the workflow trigger fires.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What Happens with Captured Data

Once a contact completes the DM sequence, Comentify saves their responses to their contact record immediately. You can view the collected data by:

1. Opening **Inbox → Contacts** and searching for the contact.
2. Clicking their name to open the contact record.
3. Reviewing the **Lead Info** section, which displays the name, email, and phone number they provided.

Captured data is also available to use as personalization variables in future automations targeting that contact.

<Tip>
  Keep your data collection sequence short and conversational. Asking for one piece of information per message — rather than all fields at once — significantly increases completion rates.
</Tip>

## Compliance and Data Collection

<Warning>
  Only collect information that you have a legitimate, disclosed reason to use. Sending unsolicited DMs to harvest personal data may violate Meta's Platform Policies and applicable privacy laws such as GDPR and CCPA. Review [Meta's Platform Terms](https://developers.facebook.com/terms) and ensure your data collection practices align with your privacy policy before activating lead capture workflows.
</Warning>

Best practices for compliant lead capture:

- Clearly state in your post or story why you're asking for information (for example, "DM us the word INFO to get our free guide — we'll ask for your email to send it").
- Only enable the fields your workflow genuinely requires.
- Honor any opt-out requests promptly by removing or anonymizing the contact's data.
