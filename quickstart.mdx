This guide takes you from a new account to your first live automation.

- Someone comments a word like **price** on your post.
- Comentify sends them a **direct message (DM)** with your reply.
- You do not have to reply by hand.

An **automation** is a rule you set once. After that, Comentify follows the rule for you.

## Before you start

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="An Instagram Business or Creator account" icon="camera">
    - Comentify does not work with a personal Instagram account.
    - A Business or Creator account is free.
    - To switch, open the Instagram app and go to **Settings** → **Account type**.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Messages from connected tools are allowed" icon="message-square">
    - Instagram must allow outside tools to send messages for you.
    - In the Instagram app, open your profile and tap the menu (☰) at the top right.
    - Tap **Messages and story replies** → **Message controls**.
    - Under **Connected tools**, turn on **Allow access to messages**.
    - Can't find it? Type **connected tools** in the search box at the top of the Instagram settings.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="No other automation tool is connected" icon="unplug">
    - Two tools can reply to the same comment and confuse your followers.
    - Disconnect any other comment or DM tool before you connect Comentify.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="A Facebook Page (only if you connect with Facebook)" icon="facebook">
    You need these only if you choose **Connect with Facebook** in step 3:
    - Your Instagram account is linked to a Facebook Page.
    - You are a full admin of that Page. Editor or Moderator access is not enough.
    - Messaging is turned on for the Page.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="A second Instagram account for testing" icon="users">
    - Comentify does not reply to comments from your own account.
    - To test your automation, you need a friend's account or a second account of your own.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Steps

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create your workspace" icon="user-plus">
    Your **workspace** is your own space in Comentify. Your accounts, contacts and automations all live there.

    1. Go to [comentify.io](https://comentify.io) and click **Get started**.
    2. Fill in the three tabs: **Personal Information**, **Company Information** and **Security**.
    3. On the **Personal Information** tab, choose your plan.
    4. Click **Create Account**.
    5. Sign in with your email and password.

    Comentify asks you to verify your email, open the email we sent and click the link.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Activate your plan" icon="credit-card">
    - If you chose a free plan, you go straight to your dashboard.
    - If you chose a paid plan, you see the **Complete Payment** page first.
    - Choose a payment method, pay, and your dashboard opens.
    - If you choose **Offline Payment**, enter your transaction details and click **Submit for approval**. Your workspace opens after our team checks the payment.

    Details: [Plans](/account/plans)
  </Step>

  <Step title="Connect your Instagram account" icon="plug">
    In Comentify, each connected Instagram account or Facebook Page is called a **channel**.

    1. In the left menu, click **Channels**.
    2. Click **Connect Channel**.
    3. Read the **Before you connect** list and check each item.
    4. Click **Connect with Instagram** or **Connect with Facebook**.
    5. Sign in to Meta's own window and allow every permission it asks for.

    When you come back, you see the message **Channel connected.**

    <Tip>
      Not sure which button to pick? Choose **Connect with Instagram** if you only use Instagram. Choose **Connect with Facebook** if you also want to answer Facebook Messenger.
    </Tip>

    Details: [Connect your accounts](/channels/connect-accounts)
  </Step>

  <Step title="Create your first automation" icon="zap">
    1. In the left menu, click **Automations**.
    2. Click **Add automation**.

    **Trigger & matching**

    - **Name:** give it a name you will remember, like "Price reply".
    - **Channel:** choose the account you just connected.
    - **Trigger:** choose **Comment → DM**.
    - **Scope:** choose **All posts**, or choose **A specific post** and pick one post.
    - **Match mode:** choose **Contains keyword**.
    - **Keyword:** type a word, like `price`, and press Enter.
    - Click **Continue**.

    **Content**

    - Write the message you want to send.
    - Type `@` to add the person's name, like `{{first_name}}`.
    - Click **Continue**.

    **Review**

    - Check the summary.
    - Leave **Turn on now** switched on.
    - Click **Save**.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Test it" icon="circle-check">
    1. Use your second Instagram account.
    2. Comment your keyword on the post you chose.
    3. Open the DMs of that second account.

    You should see your message there.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Check>
  You created a workspace, connected Instagram, and turned on an automation that answers comments with a DM.
</Check>

## Common questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Will the person also get a public reply under their comment?">
    - No. A **Comment → DM** automation only sends a DM.
    - To post a public reply as well, create a second automation and choose **Comment auto-reply** as the trigger.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Why did I not get a DM when I commented on my own post?">
    - Comentify does not reply to comments from your own account.
    - Test with a different Instagram account.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Will the same person get the DM again if they comment again?">
    - Instagram allows only one private reply to each comment.
    - A new comment with the keyword gets a new DM.
    - To answer only the first comment from each person, turn on **Only on first interaction** in the **Trigger & matching** step.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
