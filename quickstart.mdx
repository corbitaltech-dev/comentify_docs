Comentify turns your Instagram and Facebook engagement into an automated, scalable workflow — from replying to comments and sending DMs to capturing leads and tracking performance. This guide walks you through everything you need to go from a brand-new account to your first live automation in under ten minutes.

<Note>
  Comentify requires an **Instagram Professional account** (Business or Creator) or a **Facebook Page**. Personal Instagram or Facebook profiles are not supported. If you haven't already, [convert your Instagram profile to a Professional account](https://help.instagram.com/502981923235522) before continuing.
</Note>

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create your Comentify account">
    Head to [comentify.io](https://comentify.io) and sign up for a free account. Enter your email address, choose a password, and verify your email when the confirmation message arrives. Once verified, you'll land on your Comentify dashboard and be ready to connect a social account.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Connect your Instagram or Facebook account">
    From the dashboard, navigate to **Settings → Connected Accounts** and click **Connect Instagram** or **Connect Facebook Page**. You'll be redirected to Meta's secure OAuth authorization screen — log in with the Facebook account that owns your Page or is linked to your Instagram Professional profile, grant the requested permissions, and select the specific account or Page you want to manage. Comentify never stores your Meta credentials. See [Connect Accounts](/connecting-accounts) for a full walkthrough.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Create your first comment automation rule">
    Go to **Automation → Comment Replies** in the left sidebar and click **New Rule**. Give your rule a name, then configure the three core elements:

    - **Trigger** — choose a post or set a keyword filter (e.g., reply to any comment containing "price").
    - **Reply** — write the public comment reply Comentify will post automatically. Use variables like `{{first_name}}` to personalize responses.
    - **Action** (optional) — add a follow-up DM, tag a lead, or enroll the commenter in a workflow.

    Click **Save & Activate** when you're happy with the rule.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Test your automation">
    Open the post you selected in the previous step on Instagram or Facebook and leave a test comment that matches your trigger keyword. Within a few seconds, Comentify should post the reply you configured. Head back to **Automation → Comment Replies**, open your rule, and check the **Activity** tab to confirm the event was captured and the reply was sent successfully.
  </Step>
</Steps>
