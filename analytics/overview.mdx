Comentify's analytics dashboard gives you a single place to measure everything that matters — from how often your automations fire to how quickly your team closes conversations. Use these insights to fine-tune your workflows, spot your best-performing content, and prove the impact of your social engagement strategy.

## What the Analytics Dashboard Shows

The dashboard is organized into four metric areas so you can quickly find the numbers most relevant to your goals.

## How to Access Analytics

Navigate to **Analytics** in the main left-hand navigation. The dashboard loads with data for the last 30 days by default. If you manage multiple connected accounts, use the account selector at the top of the page to filter the view to a specific profile.

## Filter by Date Range

Use the date range picker in the top-right corner of the dashboard to change the reporting window. You can select a preset range — last 7 days, last 30 days, last 90 days — or define a custom start and end date. All metric cards and charts update instantly when you apply a new range.

<Note>
  Analytics data updates with a short delay and does not reflect activity in real time. Very recent automation triggers or conversations may not appear immediately in the dashboard.
</Note>

<Tip>
  Check the **Automation Performance** breakdown by post to identify which pieces of content are triggering the most automations. Posts with high trigger volume are strong candidates for A/B testing your reply scripts or lead-capture sequences.
</Tip>

## Exporting Data

You can export dashboard data as a CSV file for use in external reporting tools or spreadsheets.

<Note>
  Data export is available on higher-tier plans. If the **Export** button is not visible on your dashboard, upgrade your plan or contact your account owner to check your current tier.
</Note>

To export, set your desired date range, then click **Export** in the top-right corner of the dashboard. The CSV file includes all metric categories currently visible in your filtered view.
